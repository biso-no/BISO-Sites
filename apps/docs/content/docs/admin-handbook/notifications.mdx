# Notifications

The bell icon at the top right of every admin screen is your notification centre. It tells you when something needs your attention without forcing you to dig through every list.

## What the bell shows

A small badge appears on the bell when you have unread notifications. Click the bell to open the notifications panel. Each notification shows:

- A short title (for example "New job application")
- A timestamp
- A link into the relevant screen

Click a notification to jump to the thing it is about. Doing so marks it as read.

## Categories

There are three kinds of notification:

| Category | When you get one |
|----------|------------------|
| **New applications** | Somebody applied to a job you are responsible for, or submitted a form that landed in a queue you can access. |
| **Pending approvals** | An approval request was created and you are in the team that can approve it. |
| **System alerts** | Something on the platform needs admin attention — for example a failed integration sync or a recurring task that did not complete. |

Whether you receive a given category at all depends on your role and on your notification settings.

## Marking notifications as read

You have two options:

- **One at a time** — click a notification. It opens the linked page and is marked as read.
- **All at once** — open the notifications panel and click **Mark all as read** at the top.

Notifications are not deleted when read; they just stop counting toward the badge. The panel still shows recent items so you can scroll back through history.

## Controlling what you get

You can mute categories you don't care about from [Settings](/docs/admin-handbook/settings) (global admin) or from your personal profile menu. For example you might disable system alerts if you are not on the IT rota.

## Why a notification might be missing

A few common reasons a notification you expected didn't show up:

- The action happened on a campus you don't have access to. Campus admins only see notifications for their own campus.
- The category was muted in your settings.
- The thing that triggered it is configured to notify a different team. For example a job application goes to the team listed on the job, not necessarily to you.
- You signed out and back in around the time the event happened. Try refreshing the dashboard.
