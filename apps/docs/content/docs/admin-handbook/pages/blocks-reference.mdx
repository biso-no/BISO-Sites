# Blocks reference

The block palette is split into categories. Below is the full list, 30 blocks in total, with a short description of each. Use this page as a lookup, not a tutorial: for how to actually add and edit blocks, see [Creating and editing](/docs/admin-handbook/pages/creating-and-editing).

The top of a page is not a block. Every page has a **header** (title, intro, optional photo and buttons) that you edit above the sections.

## Side-by-side layouts

There is no two-column block. To put things next to each other, give the section **columns** (two, three or four) and drop blocks into each column. On a phone the columns stack. A section can also have a white, tinted, dark or photo background; text on dark and photo backgrounds stays readable on its own.

## Top of page

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `marquee` | A calm strip of short phrases. | Good for an announcement above or below the header. It moves slowly, and stands still for visitors who have asked their device to reduce motion. Keep the text short. |

## Tell a story

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `text` | Headings, paragraphs, bullet lists, links, bold and italic. | The main way to write prose. |
| `heading` | A section title with an optional link. | Use it to introduce a group of blocks. |
| `quote` | A pulled quotation with attribution. | Testimonials, or a line worth standing on its own. |
| `callout` | A box that draws attention to one piece of information. | One or two per page is plenty. |

## Show people and numbers

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `team` | People with photo, name, role and contact. | Type the people in yourself, or switch to **board** to show the department's current board automatically. |
| `stats` | Three or four big numbers with labels. | "3 200 members", "12 events". |
| `highlights` | Short points, each with an accent rule. | Lighter than cards when there are no links or images. |
| `facts` | Label and value rows. | Dates, places, prices. |
| `timeline` | Dated entries with a label, title and text. | Programmes ("Dag 1, 10:00") and histories ("2019"). |
| `divider` | A line between blocks. | Small, medium or large spacing. |

## Media

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `image` | A single photo with caption. | Always fill in alt text. It is how screen readers describe the image. |
| `gallery` | A grid of photos, clickable to enlarge. | Photos come from the BISO media library. |
| `video` | An embedded video. | Accepts an uploaded file or a YouTube or Vimeo link. |

## Pull from BISO

These blocks read live data, so you don't copy anything by hand. Pick a department in the inspector.

| Block | What it does | Source |
|-------|--------------|--------|
| `events` | Upcoming events as cards. | The department's events. Choose three or six. |
| `jobs` | Open roles. | The department's job board. |
| `news` | Latest posts. | The department's news. |
| `productGrid` | A department's shop products. | The department's products. |
| `departmentGrid` | Unit cards. | One campus or all campuses. |
| `partners` | A logo grid. | The partner list, or logos you add yourself. |

<Callout type="info">
If a live block has nothing to show (a department with no upcoming events, say), it **disappears from the public page**. No empty heading or placeholder is left behind, and a section that ends up empty takes no space. In the editor you still see the block with a note explaining why it is empty.
</Callout>

## Engage

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `buttons` | A row of up to three links. | |
| `ctaBand` | A headline with up to two buttons. | Use at the bottom of a page to nudge visitors to apply, sign up or visit another page. |
| `faq` | Questions that open and close. | Good for membership pages and event info. |
| `contact` | Email, address, opening hours and socials. | For offices and departments. |
| `signup` | A short form that collects emails or interest. | See the form rules below. |
| `multiStepForm` | A form in several steps. | Supports text, choice (radio) and checkbox questions. See the form rules below. |

### Form rules

`signup` and `multiStepForm` send their answers by email to the recipient you set.

- The **recipient must be a `@biso.no` address**. Other addresses are refused.
- What is sent is taken from the **published** page, not from what the visitor's browser says. If you change a form, the change only takes effect after you publish.
- When rate limiting is configured, one visitor can submit **5 times per minute**. After that they are asked to wait.

## Layout

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `cards` | Cards with title, text, optional image and link, as a grid or a scrolling row. | Replaces the old feature grid, link tiles, featured cards and scroll row. Cards no longer carry icons. |
| `tabs` | Content in tabs. | Use sparingly. Visitors often miss what is hidden in a tab. |
| `stepGrid` | Numbered steps ("1. Sign up. 2. Pick a department."). | For walkthroughs and "how it works". |

## Data

| Block | What it does | Notes |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| `documents` | A list of downloadable files. | Upload PDF, Office, CSV or TXT files, up to **25 MB** each. Visitors get a real download link. |

## Removed blocks

These no longer exist in the palette, so you can't add them.

- `profileHeader`, `filterBar` and `campusSelector`: removed, with no replacement.
- `twoCol`: use section columns instead.
- Feature grid, link tiles, featured cards and scroll row: use `cards`.

For the technical side (how blocks are defined, validated and rendered on the public site), see [Editor architecture](/docs/architecture/editor-architecture).
