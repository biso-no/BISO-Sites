# Interviews and booking

Once a candidate is in the **interview** stage of the [applications workspace](/docs/admin-handbook/jobs/applications-workspace), there are two ways to get a time in the calendar. Both end the same way: an Outlook invite goes to the candidate and the panel, with a Teams meeting attached for online interviews.

## Scheduling it yourself

Open the candidate and choose **Schedule**. The modal asks for:

- **Round** and **duration**.
- **Location**: Teams, Nydalen (in person) or Hybrid. Teams and Hybrid get a Teams meeting; every location gets an Outlook invite.
- **Panel**: the reviewers to invite. Only reviewers with an email address can be selected.

**Check availability** reads the selected panel's Outlook calendars for the next two weeks and highlights the hours where everyone is free, in Oslo office hours (09:00–17:00, weekdays). Pick an hour and confirm.

On confirm, the interview is saved, the candidate is moved to the interview stage, and the invite is sent from the lead panelist's mailbox. If the calendar connection is missing or Outlook refuses the invite, the interview is still saved and the modal tells you that no invite went out, so you can send one by hand.

## Letting the candidate pick

The **Interviews** tab has **Send a booking link**. Choose the candidate, the panel and the duration, and generate the link. Copy it into your email to the candidate; the system does not send that email.

The link opens a page on biso.no where the candidate sees only the slots where the whole panel is free, in office hours, never sooner than two hours ahead. When they confirm:

1. The interview is created with the candidate and the panel as participants.
2. A Teams meeting is created and the Outlook invite goes out to everyone.
3. The link is used up; it cannot book a second interview.

The candidate's confirmation page says whether the invite was sent. If the calendar connection is down, the slot is still recorded and the page tells them BISO will send the details.

Links expire after 14 days.

## Keeping track

The **Interviews** tab lists scheduled and proposed interviews, the panel calendar for the next ten working days, and the scorecards still outstanding. Panelists on an interview can write its scorecard; see the candidate drawer's Interviews tab for each round.

## When things go wrong

- **"Calendar is not connected."** The admin app has no Azure credentials for Microsoft Graph. IT sets `AZURE_GRAPH_CLIENT_SECRET` together with the app id and tenant id already in use by the IT module.
- **The invite did not arrive.** It is sent from the lead panelist's mailbox, so that person must have a Microsoft 365 mailbox in BISO's tenant. Check the warning shown when the interview was scheduled.
- **No free slots.** Everyone selected is busy in office hours for the whole window. Pick a smaller panel or schedule by hand.
- **The candidate's link says it was already used.** One link books one interview. Generate a new link from the Interviews tab if they need to rebook.

## Who can do this

| Role | Can schedule and issue links for |
|------|----------------------------------|
| Global admin | Any vacancy on any campus. |
| Campus admin | Any vacancy in their campus. |
| Department | Their own department's vacancies. |
