# Admin Handbook

This handbook is for the people who actually use the BISO admin app: IT staff, campus leadership, department leads, and editors. It explains what each screen does, who can see it, and how the most common tasks work.

The admin app lives at **admin.biso.no** and is separate from the public student site at biso.no. You sign in with your BI Microsoft account, and the role you get is decided by which Azure AD security groups you belong to — there is nothing to assign manually inside the app.

## Conventions used in this handbook

- **Global admin** means BISO IT and the national operations unit. They can do anything on any campus.
- **Campus admin** means the leadership team for a specific campus (Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, Stavanger). They can do anything **inside their campus**.
- **Department** means a BISO unit lead (PR, HR, Finance, Marketing, etc.). They can manage their own jobs, events, news, and pages — nothing else.

If you ever wonder "should I be able to see this?" the answer almost always comes down to your Azure AD groups. Ask BISO IT to check what groups you are in — that decides everything else.
