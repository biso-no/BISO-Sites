<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- Open **Utlegg** (Expenses) in the sidebar. The page lists the reimbursements you may see. Opening one shows its receipts, its approval steps and its history.
- What you may do depends on who you are:

| You are | You see | You can do |
|---|---|---|
| Global admin | Every reimbursement | Everything below, on any step |
| Campus controller (the active controller in the approver directory) | Reimbursements on your campus | Edit, approve or reject on behalf of any step, change a department step's approver, retry a failed booking. Never on a reimbursement the student said they sent as the controller. |
| Campus leader (Ledelsen) | Reimbursements on your campus | Read only |
| Approver on a step, with a Microsoft account | Reimbursements where you are an approver | Edit, approve or reject while your step is the current one |
| Role mailbox with no teams | Only **Utlegg** | The same as an approver |

- Nobody can act on their own reimbursement, whatever their role.
- Only the current step can be decided, edited by its approver, or have its approver changed. A reimbursement is decided one step at a time.
- Deciding, changing the approver, retrying and rebuilding an approval chain need **Direct ledger posting (24SO + Teams approval)** to be on. See [System settings](/guide/access-and-settings/system-settings#switch-a-feature-flag).
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the reimbursement">

The list has three views: **Alle** (All), **Venter på meg** (Waiting on me) and **Trenger oppfølging** (Needs attention). You can search by number or description and filter by **Status**, **Campus** and **Avdeling** (Department). The status is **Innsendt** (Submitted), **Venter på godkjenning** (Waiting for approval), **Godkjent** (Approved), **Bokført** (Booked), **Avslått** (Rejected) or **Bokføring feilet** (Booking failed).

A reimbursement that has just been submitted shows a **Sjekker** (Checking) badge next to its status while the AI check is still working. See [What happens after a student submits](#what-happens-after-a-student-submits).

**Trenger oppfølging** shows reimbursements whose booking failed and those with an open approval issue. Open issues are listed above the table. See [Fix expense approval issues](/guide/access-and-settings/expense-approvals).

</Step>
<Step title="Check the receipts">

Choose a reimbursement. Each receipt shows its date, description, amount, type and account. Choose **Åpne** (Open) to see the file in a new tab.

The AI check has already looked at the receipt. Under it you may see:

- A line that starts **AI-sjekk:** (AI check). It is the check's own reason for the type, the account or the project it set, in one sentence. If the check only raised a doubt, the line shows the doubt, unless a note for that receipt under **Ting å sjekke** says the same.
- **Ting å sjekke** (Things to check): what the AI or the system is unsure about. Besides a missing account, an old receipt or a possible duplicate, the check can say that it was not sure of the type, that it was not sure of the project, that no project was found, that the project for next year's edition has not been created in Finago yet, or that the student chose a type the AI would not have picked and suggests another. If the check did not finish, a note says the AI has not looked at everything on this expense.

These notes are for you and the approvers. The student never sees them.

A check never changes a type, account or project that the student, an approver or an admin has chosen. If the check disagrees with a choice, it only writes a note.

The full bank account is shown only to those who can edit it. Everyone else sees the last four digits.

</Step>
<Step title="Fix what is wrong">

If you may edit, choose **Rediger** (Edit) on a receipt. You can change the type, the account, the project, the date, the description and the amount. See [Finago projects on reimbursements](/guide/access-and-settings/expense-projects). Choosing a type fills in the type's account. Choosing an account fills in that account's tax code. Global admins and the controller can also choose **Rediger bankkonto** (Edit bank account): 11 digits, and spaces and dots are ignored. The number must also pass the Norwegian check digit test, so a mistyped digit is refused.

Every change is saved in **Historikk** (History) with the old and new value.

A reimbursement that is approved but not yet booked can't be edited, because booking may start at any moment. One whose booking failed can be edited by global admins and the controller, so they can fix the cause before retrying.

</Step>
<Step title="Approve or reject">

When it's your turn, the **Avgjørelse** (Decision) box shows **Godkjenn** (Approve) and **Avslå** (Reject). Rejecting asks for **Begrunnelse** (Reason), which the student sees.

A global admin or the controller who isn't the approver on the step sees **Godkjenn på vegne av** (Approve on behalf of) and **Avslå på vegne av** (Reject on behalf of), with the role's name. The step is recorded as decided on behalf of that role, and the history says so.

Approve is refused while a receipt has no ledger account. You see **En kvittering mangler konto. Velg konto før du godkjenner.** (A receipt has no ledger account. Set the account before approving.) Set the account on that receipt in the admin with **Rediger** (Edit), then approve. Approve and reject are also refused once the reimbursement is no longer waiting for approval. You see **Dette utlegget venter ikke lenger på godkjenning.** (This reimbursement is no longer waiting for approval.) That is checked first, so it is what you see on a decided reimbursement even when a receipt has no account.

The decision goes through the same chain as an approval in Teams. The next step's approver is told, and the last approval starts the booking.

</Step>
<Step title="Change the approver">

Choose **Bytt godkjenner** (Change approver) and type the new person's address. It must end in `@biso.no`, and it can't be the student who submitted. Choose **Bytt og send** (Change and send).

The new approver gets a fresh approval link and the old link stops working. Changing the general manager's step, or a step where the general manager stands in for a missing president or controller, changes it for this reimbursement only. The general manager is set under **Utlegg**, **Innstillinger**, **Godkjennere**. Other changes also update the saved approver for later reimbursements with the same role, so check you mean it. Global admins can change any step. The controller can change department steps only, not the controller or president step.

A global admin also sees **Bygg godkjenningsflyten på nytt** (Rebuild approval chain) on a reimbursement that is waiting for approval with no approval steps, because no approver could be found or because building the steps failed after the AI check. Such a reimbursement has an approval issue that starts **No approver could be resolved** or **The approval chain could not be created**. The button shows only while **Direct ledger posting (24SO + Teams approval)** is on. A reimbursement sent to accounting by email, while the flag was off, also waits with no steps, but it has no such issue and never gets the button: accounting pays it, and approving it here as well would pay the student twice. Set up the approvers first. Rebuilding builds the steps again, and the history records it. If it is refused, you see **Bare et utlegg som sto uten godkjenningsflyt da det ble levert, kan få godkjenningsflyten bygget på nytt.** (Only a reimbursement left without an approval chain when it was submitted can have its approval chain rebuilt.)

</Step>
<Step title="Retry a failed booking">

A reimbursement with **Bokføring feilet** (Booking failed) shows the error from 24SevenOffice and **Prøv bokføring på nytt** (Retry booking).

When the booking failed after 24SevenOffice had already accepted it and this system recorded that, Retry doesn't book again. It finds the booking, saves the status and the transaction number, and the reimbursement shows **Bokført** (Booked). When the booking was never recorded here (the message with the transaction number below), a retry would book it a second time.

Sometimes 24SevenOffice accepts a booking but the admin can't save that. Then you see **Finago har bokføringen, men statusen kunne ikke lagres. Ikke bokfør utlegget på nytt: når det viser «Bokføring feilet» (innen omtrent 15 minutter), fullfører «Prøv bokføring på nytt» lagringen.** (Finago has the booking, but saving the status failed. Don't book it again: once the reimbursement shows Booking failed (within about 15 minutes), Retry finishes saving it.) Don't book it in 24SevenOffice by hand. Wait until the reimbursement shows **Bokføring feilet** (Booking failed), then choose **Prøv bokføring på nytt** (Retry booking): it only finishes the save.

If you see **Finago har bokføringen (\{transactionId}), men den kunne ikke lagres her. Ikke prøv på nytt; kontakt IT.** (Finago has the booking (\{transactionId}) but it could not be saved here. Do not retry; contact IT.), the admin couldn't save the booking anywhere, so a retry would book it again. Don't retry. Give IT the transaction number in the message.

Otherwise open 24SevenOffice first and check that the reimbursement isn't already there. If it is booked twice, the student is paid twice. When the admin has a booking recorded for the reimbursement, Retry only saves it or refuses, but the admin can't see 24SevenOffice itself.

Choose **Prøv på nytt** (Retry). You see **Bokført i Finago** (Booked in Finago) when it works, **Bokføringen mislyktes igjen** (The booking failed again) when it doesn't, and **Bokføringen pågår** (The booking is in progress) when another run holds it. In that case check 24SevenOffice before you do anything else.

</Step>
</Steps>

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- After a student submits, the AI check runs first. The approvers are told when it is done, normally within a minute. See [What happens after a student submits](#what-happens-after-a-student-submits).
- After a decision, the reimbursement moves to the next step, or to booking after the last approval. Booking runs on its own, without anyone starting it.
- A successful booking shows **Bokført** (Booked) and the transaction number in 24SevenOffice.
- A type an approver or admin changes teaches the expense AI, and a rejected reimbursement is forgotten by it. See [Manage expense types](/guide/access-and-settings/expense-types).
- A failed booking is never retried by itself, because 24SevenOffice may already have it. Marking a stuck one as failed is not a retry.
- A booking that stopped without finishing is marked **Bokføring feilet** (Booking failed) the next time the scheduled run meets it, once the claim is older than 15 minutes, so it doesn't stay stuck. It's only marked failed, not retried. Booking falls back to the department stored on the reimbursement itself when its department link is missing.
- Edits, decisions, approver changes and retries are written to the reimbursement's history with your name.
- After a decision made in the admin, the next approver gets the Teams card and the email, and a rejection notifies the student.
- When a step is decided in the admin or on the web approval page, the Teams card that was sent to that step's approver changes to show who decided and what. The buttons go away. This happens after the reimbursement has moved on, so a slow Teams never holds it up. A decision made in Teams already updates its own card. If the card can't be updated within 5 seconds, it stays as it was, and the decision still stands.
</WhatHappensNext>

## What happens after a student submits

The student gets their answer at once, and the reimbursement shows **Venter på godkjenning** (Waiting for approval). Then the AI check starts in the background. It reads each receipt and suggests a type, an account for the student's own text, and a Finago project. A program decides what to keep. It keeps a suggestion only if the type, account or project exists and can be used, and it never overrides a choice made by a person.

- While the check runs, the reimbursement shows a **Sjekker** (Checking) badge and has no approval steps yet.
- When the check is done, the approvers are told. This is the first time anyone but the student hears about it.
- The check is careful with projects. A suggestion below 80 percent sure is kept as a note, not as a project. A project from last year is never used. If the project for this year's edition hasn't been created in Finago yet, the receipt gets a note that says so, and finance can create the project. Costs just after an event go to the edition that has just finished.
- If the check fails or runs out of time, the approvers are still told. Their notice then says that the AI check did not finish, and the usual fixed steps have set what they could.
- While **Direct ledger posting (24SO + Teams approval)** is off, the check ends with an email to accounting instead. The email and its PDF cover sheet list type, account and project for each receipt, then the occasion, the event and the notes from the check.
- A reimbursement can stay on **Sjekker** if something went wrong. A job that runs every few minutes finishes checks that are more than 10 minutes old. If the badge is still there after about 20 minutes, tell IT.

## What the approver sees in Teams and email

The approval card in Teams, and the email, give the approver what they need to decide without opening the admin. The card is in English.

- **Receipts.** A table with the date, description, type, account and amount of each receipt. The card shows up to 15 receipts and the email up to 30, then **and N more**. A receipt without a type shows the student's own text in quotes.
- **Notes for the approver.** The things the AI or the system flagged, such as a missing account, an amount that differs from what the receipt reads, an old receipt or a possible duplicate. These are the same as **Ting å sjekke** (Things to check) in the admin, including what the AI check found. A note about one receipt starts with that receipt's description, or its type when it has none, cut to 40 characters.
- **The check's reason.** Under a receipt the check set a type, account or project on, one short line says why. A receipt the check only flagged shows the line when no note already says the same.
- **The submitter's declaration.** A line when the student said they are the campus controller or the department's financial manager.
- **The national note.** It shows when the general manager approves in someone else's place. On National, when the controller can't be found or is the one who submitted, it reads **The controller could not be found or is the submitter; the general manager decides instead.** On the four campuses it shows when the general manager approves a reimbursement. For a controller's own reimbursement it reads **The campus president could not be found or is the submitter; the general manager decides instead.** Otherwise, when the general manager stands in for a controller who submitted, it reads **The general manager decides in place of the usual approver.**
- **Recent history.** The latest 6 history lines. If the card gets too large for Teams, the history goes first, then the receipts are cut to 5, and as a last resort the card shows only the number of receipts.
- **Buttons.** **Approve**, **Reject**, **Open in admin** and **View receipts**. When a receipt has no account, or the receipts couldn't be read, **Approve** is replaced by **Set the account in admin**, which opens the reimbursement. Set the account on the receipt, then approve in the admin. The email has the same buttons.
- **The approval page.** The email's link opens a page on the website. When a receipt has no account, the page has no **Godkjenn** (Approve) button. It shows **En kvittering mangler konto. Sett den i admin før du godkjenner.** (A receipt has no account yet. Set it in admin before approving.) with the link **Åpne i admin** (Open in admin). **Avslå** (Reject) stays. An old card or link that still tries to approve is refused with the same sentence, unless the reimbursement is no longer waiting for approval: then it is refused for that.

## What the student sees

The student sees the status and, after a rejection, your reason. They never see the history or who edited what.

Their reimbursement is checked right after they submit, and the approvers get it a minute later. The student doesn't wait for the check, and they never see the AI check's notes, accounts or projects.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting on a reimbursement that still says **Sjekker** (Checking) after 20 minutes. Tell IT, because the check is stuck.
- Retrying a booking without checking 24SevenOffice first.
- Approving on behalf of a role when the real approver is available. The history shows it, but the approver isn't told.
- Changing the approver to fix a one-off absence. The saved approver for the role changes too, except on the general manager's step, where the dialog says the change is for this reimbursement only.
- Looking for a reimbursement on another campus as a campus leader or controller. You see your own campus only.
