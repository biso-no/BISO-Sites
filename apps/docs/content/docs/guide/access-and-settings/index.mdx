This area of the admin decides who gets in and what they can do. It's mostly for IT, which in this guide means the global admins. When a page asks you to tell the developers, it means whoever runs the platform behind the admin, as described in the [developer docs](/developers). It covers:

- **IT**, where global admins manage BISO's Microsoft accounts: creating people, changing their groups, resetting passwords and handing over role accounts.
- The rest of the **System** group in the sidebar: **Aktivitet** (Activity), **Varsling** (Whistleblowing), **Driftshelse** (Operations health), **Funksjonsbrytere** (Feature flags), **Appversjoner** (App versions), **Betaling** (Payments) and **Innstillinger** (Settings).

The activity log is covered in [Everyday tools](/guide/start-here/everyday-tools#check-the-activity-log-global-admins-and-campus-leaders).

## Where roles come from

Your role in the admin comes from the BISO Microsoft account you sign in with. IT puts each account in Microsoft groups whose names start with `SG-App-`: usually one for the campus and one for the department. The admin reads those groups each time you sign in and gives you the role that matches.

| Role | What gives it |
|---|---|
| Global admins (National + Operations Unit) | The National campus group and the Operations Unit department group |
| Campus leaders (Ledelsen on your campus) | A city's campus group and that city's Ledelsen group, for Oslo, Bergen, Stavanger or Trondheim |
| National HR | The National campus group and the HR department group |
| HR | The HR department group |
| Department members | A department group. IT normally adds the campus group too. |
| Benefits editors | IT's benefits editor list. This role doesn't come from a group. |

- A change to someone's groups takes effect at their next sign-in. If IT changes your groups while you're signed in, sign out and sign in again.
- To become a benefits editor, ask IT to add you to the benefits editor list, then sign in as usual. See [How membership and benefits work](/guide/members-and-benefits#who-can-use-members-and-benefits).
- Global admins add and remove groups on a person's page in **IT**. See [Manage Microsoft users](/guide/access-and-settings/manage-users).

## Who can use access and settings

| Page | Global admins (National + Operations Unit) | Campus leaders (Ledelsen on your campus) | Everyone else |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IT**, the **Brukere** (Users) tab | Every account, and every change | Accounts on your own campus, read-only | No |
| **IT**, the **Avdelingskontroll** (Department audit), **24SO datakvalitet** (24SO data health) and **Godkjenningsavvik** (Approval issues) tabs | Yes | No | No |
| Handing over a role account | Yes | No | No |
| **Aktivitet** (Activity) | Yes | Yes | No |
| **Varsling** (Whistleblowing) | Yes | No | No |
| **Innstillinger** (Settings), **Driftshelse** (Operations health), **Funksjonsbrytere** (Feature flags), **Appversjoner** (App versions) and **Betaling** (Payments) | Yes | No | No |

Opening a page your role can't use shows a page-not-found error.

## Campus leaders in IT

Campus leaders have **IT** under **System** in the sidebar, with read-only access to their own campus:

- The list shows only accounts whose **Kontorsted** (Office location) is your campus. A line above the list reads **Filtrert til ditt campus** (Filtered to your campus), followed by the campus name. Accounts with no office location aren't shown.
- You can open an account and read its profile, aliases, manager, groups, licences and sign-in methods. Every field is greyed out, and there are no buttons to change anything.
- The **Risikokontroller** (Risk controls) box reads **No risk controls available for your permission level.**
- There's no **Opprett bruker** (Create user) button, and you see only the users list, with no tabs.

To change anything about an account, ask a global admin.

## Who to ask

| You need | Ask |
|---|---|
| A BISO Microsoft account, or a role in the admin | A global admin (IT) |
| A new password, or your two-step sign-in (MFA) reset | A global admin (IT) |
| To become a benefits editor | IT, who keep the benefits editor list |
| A change to an account on your campus | A global admin. Campus leaders can look, but not change. |
| A change to who gets whistleblowing reports | A global admin |
| A change to payments, feature flags or app version rules | A global admin |

## What the system does on its own

- Reads your groups each time you sign in, and gives you the matching role.
- Writes changes made in IT, **Varsling** (Whistleblowing), **Funksjonsbrytere** (Feature flags), **Betaling** (Payments) and **Appversjoner** (App versions) to the activity log.
- Stops a role account's 7-day retention run by itself after a handover. See [Hand over a role account](/guide/access-and-settings/hand-over-a-role-account).
- Lists reimbursements whose approver couldn't be found or reached under **Godkjenningsavvik** (Approval issues). See [Fix expense approval issues](/guide/access-and-settings/expense-approvals).
- Applies feature flag changes within about 15 seconds, and app version rules the next time the app starts.

It never sends:

- an email with a new account's password, or a temporary password. Passwords are only shown on screen.
- a message to IT about a new approval issue
- an email or notification when someone's account, groups or role change

## What you do yourself

- Give people their groups when they join, and remove them when they leave. When someone leaves, also ask the developers to follow their checklist in [When the work ends](/developers/start-here/getting-access#when-the-work-ends).
- Pass temporary passwords to their owners yourself, over a secure channel.
- Open **Godkjenningsavvik** (Approval issues) now and then. Nothing tells you about new ones.
- Keep the whistleblowing contacts up to date, and send a test after each change.
- Run the department check, and fix department faults in 24SevenOffice.
