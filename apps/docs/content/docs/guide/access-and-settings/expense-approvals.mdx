## Before you start

- This list matters only while the feature flag **Direct ledger posting (24SO + Teams approval)** is on. Then each reimbursement a student submits goes to one or more approvers, who approve it in Teams or by email, and is posted to 24SevenOffice. While the flag is off, reimbursements are emailed to accounting instead, and no new issues are recorded. See [System settings](/guide/access-and-settings/system-settings#switch-a-feature-flag).
- Approvers are BISO role accounts in Microsoft 365, and the reimbursements end up in 24SevenOffice. You can't see either from this guide, so the steps are text only.
- Who approves depends on the campus and the department:
  - **Oslo, ordinary departments:** the department's financial manager approves first, then the campus controller.
  - **Drift Campus and Ledelsen, on every campus:** the campus controller approves, in one step. There's no department step.
  - **Bergen, Trondheim and Stavanger, other departments:** the campus controller approves.
  - **National:** the national manager's mailbox approves.
- The student can say who they are. On an Oslo department they can switch on **Jeg er økonomiansvarlig for denne enheten** (I am the financial manager of this department), and the department's manager approves in their place, with the deputy as the next choice. On Drift Campus and Ledelsen they can switch on **Jeg er campusets controller** (I'm the campus financial controller), and the campus president approves instead. The admin can't check either claim. It takes the student's word. A switch the department doesn't offer is ignored.
- A reimbursement never goes to the controller it came from. If no president can be found for a controller's own reimbursement, the national manager's mailbox approves, and an issue is recorded.
- The admin keeps an approver directory, the table `expense_approvers`, with one entry per approver role: a department's financial manager, manager or deputy, or a campus's controller, president or the national approver. It looks in the directory first. If the entry isn't there, it asks the AI to choose among the matching Microsoft 365 accounts, for a department role or a president. It uses the answer only when the AI is at least 80 percent sure, and saves it to the directory for next time. For a campus controller and the national approver there's no AI step. The admin saves BISO's standard role mailbox on the first reimbursement that needs it and uses that. Global admins and campus admins edit the directory under **Utlegg** (Expenses), **Innstillinger** (Settings), **Godkjennere** (Approvers). See [The approver directory](#the-approver-directory).
- A directory entry becomes confirmed when that approver approves a reimbursement from their own step, using the same address the entry holds. Confirmed entries are never overwritten by the AI or the standard mailbox.
- A reimbursement is never sent to the person who submitted it, and never to the same person on two steps. See the reasons below.
- The lookup finds a department's approver in two ways. It takes accounts with the department in **Avdeling** (Department) whose login address starts with a role word. It also searches by address: a role word, the campus name (oslo, bergen, trondheim or trd, stavanger) and a part of the department's name, for example `finance.oslo.<unit>`. The role words are `finance.`, `financial.` or `okonomi` for the financial manager, `manager.` or `leder.` for the manager, `deputy.`, `deputymanager.` or `nestleder.` for the deputy, and `president.` for a campus president. A president is found the same way, except that the address search needs only the role word and the campus name, with no part of the department's name.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the approval issues">

Open **Utlegg** (Expenses) in the sidebar and choose **Trenger oppfølging** (Needs attention). Open issues are listed in a box headed **Godkjenningsavvik for refusjoner** (Reimbursement approval issues), above the list of reimbursements. The old address under **IT** sends you here. If there are no open issues, the box isn't shown.

The box shows up to 200 issues, open ones first, then the newest. The columns are **Avdeling** (Department), **Campus**, **Søkt rolle** (Sought role), **Årsak** (Reason), **Opprettet** (Created) and **Status**, which is **Åpen** (Open) or **Løst** (Resolved).

You see the issues for campuses you can see: all of them as a global admin, and your own campus as a campus leader or controller. Only global admins and the campus controller of the issue's campus get the buttons **Marker som løst** (Mark resolved) and **Send varsel på nytt** (Resend notification). Campus leaders can only read.

</Step>
<Step title="Read the reason">

**Årsak** (Reason) is in English. It tells you which kind of issue it is:

- It starts **No finance approver** or **No manager approver**. The directory had no entry and the lookup found no account it was sure of for the department. Go to the next step.
- It starts **No president approver found**. A campus controller submitted a reimbursement and no campus president could be found. It went to the national manager's mailbox. Find the president's role account as in the next step, then mark the issue as resolved.
- It reads **Expense has no department set.** There's no approver account to fix. The reimbursement went to the campus controller. Mark the issue as resolved, as in [Mark an issue as resolved](#mark-an-issue-as-resolved).
- It reads **Approver is the submitter**, or `Approver is the submitter; sent to <address> instead`. The step's approver was the person who submitted the reimbursement, so the step was changed. With the **sent to** text, the step went to a stand-in: for a controller, the campus's standard mailbox or else the national manager's mailbox; for a president or the national approver, the national manager's mailbox. Without it, the step was dropped, and the next step still approves. A department step has no stand-in, so the campus controller approves. Nothing is broken. Check that the right person approves in the reimbursement's steps, and mark the issue as resolved. If the submitter is the only person the directory holds for a role, add another person in the directory.
- It reads `Same approver as the <role> step; the earlier <role> step was dropped`. One person held two steps, for example the finance manager and the controller. Only the later, stronger step is kept, so they approve once. Nothing to do unless it is wrong. If the two roles shouldn't be the same person, change one of the entries in the directory, then mark the issue as resolved.
- It reads `The <role> entry is deactivated in the directory`, for example **The finance entry is deactivated in the directory**, or **The manager and deputy entries are deactivated in the directory**. A department's approver was switched off, so the admin didn't look anyone up. The department step was skipped and the campus controller approves. To get a department approver back, switch the entry on, or save a new person, in the directory, and then mark the issue as resolved.
- It reads `The campus controller entry is deactivated in the directory; sent to the default <address>`, or the same with **national approver**. The campus's entry was switched off, but a campus always needs an approver, so the standard mailbox approves. Switch the entry back on or save the person you want, in the directory.
- It reads `The president entry is deactivated in the directory; sent to the national approver (<address>)`. A campus controller's reimbursement had no president to go to, and the president entry is switched off. The national manager's mailbox approves. Switch the entry back on in the directory, or mark the issue as resolved.
- It starts **Could not notify**. The approver was found, but the message asking them to approve couldn't be delivered. Go to [Resend the approval](#resend-the-approval).
- It starts **No approver could be resolved**. Nobody was found for any step, not even a standard mailbox, so the reimbursement waits with no approver. There's no step to resend, so the issue has no **Send varsel på nytt** (Resend notification). Report it to the developers.

A change in the directory only reaches reimbursements submitted after it. The steps of a reimbursement already submitted keep their approver. To fix the reimbursement in the issue, change the approver on its pending step with **Bytt godkjenner** (Change approver) on the reimbursement's page (see [Review and decide reimbursements](/guide/access-and-settings/expenses-admin)), or resend it if the approver is right but didn't get the message.

**Søkt rolle** (Sought role) shows which approver was wanted, as a code: `finance`, `manager`, `deputy`, `controller`, `president` or `national`.

</Step>
<Step title="Fix a missing approver">

The department's approver account doesn't have the department in **Avdeling** (Department), has a login address the lookup doesn't recognise, or doesn't exist.

1. Choose **Brukere** (Users), and find the department's financial manager role account. If **Søkt rolle** (Sought role) says `manager`, find the leader's or the deputy's account instead. If it says `president`, find the campus president's.
2. Open it, set **Avdeling** (Department) to the department named in the issue, exactly, and choose **Lagre profil** (Save profile). For a president, check that the login address starts with `president.` and has the campus name. See [Change an account's details](/guide/access-and-settings/manage-users#change-an-accounts-details-global-admins).
3. If the department has no such account, it needs one. See [Create a user](/guide/access-and-settings/manage-users#create-a-user-global-admins-and-campus-leaders).
4. Go back to **Utlegg** (Expenses), **Trenger oppfølging** (Needs attention) and choose **Marker som løst** (Mark resolved) on the issue.

</Step>
<Step title="Resend the approval">

An open issue whose reason starts **Could not notify** has **Send varsel på nytt** (Resend notification). Choose it.

The approver gets a new approval link, in Teams if possible and by email. You see **Godkjenningsvarsel sendt på nytt** (Approval notification re-sent), and the issue is marked **Løst** (Resolved).

If it fails, you see an English message with the reason. While **Direct ledger posting (24SO + Teams approval)** is off, it's always `expenses_ledger_posting disabled`.

</Step>
<Step title="Mark an issue as resolved">

Choose **Marker som løst** (Mark resolved). You see **Avviket er markert som løst** (Issue marked as resolved), and the status changes to **Løst** (Resolved).

This only changes the status. It doesn't check that the department was fixed, and it doesn't send the reimbursement anywhere. A resolved issue can't be opened again.

</Step>
</Steps>

## The approver directory

Open **Utlegg** (Expenses), **Innstillinger** (Settings) and the tab **Godkjennere** (Approvers). It lists the entries of the approver directory, one row per approver role. Filter by campus and role, or search by name, email or department. The list shows up to 500 entries. When there are more, it says so: filter by campus or role to see the rest.

**Who can edit what**

| You are | You can edit |
|---|---|
| Global admin | Every row, on every campus, at department and campus level |
| Campus admin | Rows for a department's financial manager, manager or deputy, on the campuses you manage |

The campus controller, president and national approver are for global admins only. Campus admins don't see them as choices, and the server refuses them. Being a campus controller gives no extra rights here. Everyone else doesn't see the tab. A campus admin still sees the other rows of their campuses, but with a locked switch and no **Rediger** (Edit) button.

**Columns.** **Kilde** (Source) says where the entry came from:

- **Standard** (Seed): BISO's standard role mailbox, saved by the admin the first time a reimbursement needed it.
- **AI**: chosen by the AI lookup. **AI-sikkerhet** (AI confidence) shows how sure it was. Check these rows and confirm or change them.
- **Admin**: someone saved it in this tab, or changed the approver on a reimbursement.
- **Bekreftet** (Confirmed): an AI or standard entry whose person has since approved a reimbursement on their own step.

**Sist bekreftet** (Last confirmed) is the time that person last approved a step from this entry. An **Admin** entry keeps its source when its person approves. It only gets a confirmed time. **Sist brukt** (Last used) is the last time the entry was picked for a reimbursement.

**Add or edit.** Choose **Legg til godkjenner** (Add approver), or **Rediger** (Edit) on a row. Choose the level, **Avdeling** (Department) or **Campus** (global admins only), the campus, the department for a department level, the role, a name and an email. The email must be an @biso.no address. A department level takes the financial manager, manager or deputy, and a campus level the controller, president or national manager. The national manager goes on National only. A role holds one person per department or campus: saving a second one for the same place changes the existing entry. Saving always makes the entry source **Admin** and switches it on.

A new email means a new person. Saving it clears the entry's confirmed time and AI confidence, and the admin looks the address up in Microsoft 365 so the person can get the Teams card. If the lookup finds nobody or fails, they get the approval by email only. When you change the email in the dialog, the name field empties unless you typed a name. Left empty, the entry takes the name from Microsoft 365.

**Deactivate and reactivate.** Use the switch **Aktiv** (Active) on a row. A deactivated entry means "don't use this person":

- The system never switches it on again, and the AI doesn't look the role up again. Only a person does, with the switch, by saving the entry, or by changing the approver on a reimbursement.
- A department step with a deactivated entry is skipped, so the reimbursement goes on to the campus controller. An issue is recorded, as above.
- A deactivated controller or national entry still routes to the campus's standard mailbox, because a campus must always have one. An issue is recorded.

Every save and every switch is written to the activity log with your email.

## What happens next

- An issue is recorded on its own when a reimbursement is submitted and its department approver can't be found, when no campus president can be found for a controller's own reimbursement, or when an approver can't be sent their approval link.
- A reimbursement whose department approver couldn't be found doesn't wait for you. It skips that step and goes to the campus controller. A controller's own reimbursement with no president goes to the national manager's mailbox. Fixing the account helps the reimbursements that come after it, once the lookup finds it and saves it to the directory.
- When the lookup finds nobody, it doesn't look for the same approver again for up to an hour. Until then, reimbursements fall back as above and record the same issue. So a fixed account can take up to an hour to be found.
- When a step is decided in the admin or on the web approval page, the Teams card that was sent for it changes to show the decision. See [Review and decide reimbursements](/guide/access-and-settings/expenses-admin).
- Each approver the admin finds is recorded as an **approver_resolved** event, with the role and where it came from: the directory, the AI lookup or the standard mailbox. When the national manager's mailbox stands in for a president, the event reads `national (president fallback)`. The events are kept in the table `expense_events`. No page in the admin shows them.
- A reimbursement whose approver couldn't be reached waits at that step until you choose **Send varsel på nytt** (Resend notification).
- Nobody is told about a new issue. Open the tab now and then to check.
- Marking an issue as resolved and resending are written to the activity log with your email.
</WhatHappensNext>

## Who sees what, and deciding in the admin

An approver doesn't have to use Teams or email. Everyone on a step can open the reimbursement under **Utlegg** (Expenses) and approve or reject it there, while their step is the current one. A global admin or the campus controller can also decide on behalf of a step, change an approver and retry a failed booking. Campus leaders see their campus read only. The controller never acts on a reimbursement the student said they sent as the controller. See [Review and decide reimbursements](/guide/access-and-settings/expenses-admin).

## Common mistakes

- Marking an issue as resolved before fixing the account. Nothing checks it, so the next reimbursement from that department hits the same problem.
- Expecting **Marker som løst** (Mark resolved) to send the reimbursement on. It only changes the status.
- Expecting the admin to pick up a fix at once for a reimbursement already sent. The approvers are chosen when the student submits. Use the resend only for a notification that failed.
- Expecting the next reimbursement to find a fixed account straight away. After a lookup has found nobody, the admin waits up to an hour before it looks for the same approver again.
- Setting the department on the student who submitted. Set it on the department's approver role account.
- Choosing a department with a similar name. The admin looks for the exact department name shown in the issue.
- Switching an entry off to "pause" an approver for a week. The system won't use that role again, and the AI won't look it up, until someone switches it back on. Change the approver on the reimbursement instead, or save someone else.
- Leaving a **Could not notify** issue open. That reimbursement waits until you resend.
