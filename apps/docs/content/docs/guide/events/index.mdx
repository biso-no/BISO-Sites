Events are how BISO tells students what's on. In the admin they sit under **Innhold** (Content) → **Arrangementer** (Events) in the sidebar. Students find them on biso.no/events and in the BISO app.

The events list follows your admin language. The event editor and the logistics page are in English whatever your language, so this guide names their labels in English.

BISO doesn't sell event tickets or take registrations itself. Where people buy a ticket or sign up goes in the event's ticket link, which biso.no shows as a **Kjøp billetter** (Get tickets) button. Students can also say whether they're coming with the RSVP buttons.

## Who can use events

| Who | Events | Logistics and RSVPs | Message a group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global admins (National + Operations Unit) | Every event, on any campus and National | Every event | Yes |
| Campus leaders (Ledelsen on your campus) | Events on your campus, and National events | The same events | Groups on your campus's events |
| Department members, for their own department | Your department's events on your campus | The same events | No |

- HR members count as members of their department here.
- Anyone else, such as a benefits editor who isn't in a department, doesn't have **Arrangementer** (Events) in the sidebar.
- Opening an event you can't edit shows a page-not-found error.
- Department members must choose their own department as the event's host, even for a draft. An event with no department, such as a campus-wide event or an event imported from Tickster, is only in the list for campus leaders and global admins.
- Campus leaders can move an event between their campus and National. Department members can't change the campus.
- Only global admins can switch on the notification for a National event. See [How notifications reach students](/guide/announcements).
- Messaging a group is a push announcement, so it's for global admins and campus leaders. See [Send an announcement](/guide/announcements/send-an-announcement).
- Nobody has to approve an event. If you can edit an event, you can publish it. **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) shows draft events to global admins and campus leaders, but an event doesn't have to go through it.

If you think you should have access and don't, ask IT to check your groups.

## From draft to live

An event has one status for both languages. The list shows it as a pill:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Utkast** (Draft) | Saved with **Save as draft**. Drafts aren't shown on biso.no or in the app. |
| **Publisert** (Published) | Saved with **Publish event**. It's on biso.no and in the app until it ends. |
| **Avlyst** (Cancelled) | Only an event imported from Tickster can have this status. biso.no doesn't show it. |

- **Publish event** makes the event public at once.
- To take a published event offline, open it and choose **Save as draft**.
- There's no cancel button in the admin. To call an event off, see [Run the event](/guide/events/run-the-event#change-or-cancel-the-event).
- When an event has ended, biso.no takes it off its list by itself.
- biso.no lists events for the campus the visitor chose and National. The app lists events for the one campus chosen in the app, so a National event isn't in a city campus's list there. A notification or a link still opens it.

The **Publish schedule** setting has **Publish now** and **Schedule for…**. **Schedule for…** doesn't hold the event back: it goes live as soon as you choose **Publish event**. The time you pick only delays the event's notification. To keep an event hidden until a date, save it as a draft and publish it yourself on that date.

## Tickets, RSVP and members-only

**The ticket link.** Paste the page where people buy a ticket or sign up, such as Tickster. biso.no shows it as **Kjøp billetter** (Get tickets). A paid event needs one, because biso.no has no checkout for events. The member price is shown as text on the event. The ticket seller applies it.

**RSVP.** Signed-in students can answer **Kommer** (Going), **Kanskje** (Maybe) or **Kommer ikke** (Not going) on a published event, on biso.no or in the app. Each student has one answer per event and can change or remove it until the event ends. An answer isn't a ticket, a seat or a registration. You see the answers, with names and emails, on the event's logistics page.

RSVP takes no notice of **Capacity**, **Waitlist** or **Registration closes**:

- Students can answer until the event ends, even after **Registration closes** and with any number already going.
- **Capacity** is shown on biso.no as a number of places. It doesn't limit answers or tickets.
- **Waitlist** has no effect outside the editor.
- The **Påmeldte** (Registered) column in **Arrangementer** (Events) always reads 0. RSVPs aren't counted there.

**Members only.** A members-only event is still shown to everyone, on biso.no and in the app, marked as members-only. It changes two things:

- Only BISO members can answer RSVP.
- In the app, the ticket button is locked for students who aren't members. biso.no shows **Kjøp billetter** (Get tickets) to everyone.

## Events imported from Tickster

When the Tickster import runs, it copies the events that BISO's campuses sell on tickster.com into **Arrangementer** (Events). When it's set up, it runs every few minutes. Nobody starts it from the admin, and no screen shows that it ran.

What the import does with each event depends on its sale at Tickster:

| At Tickster | In the admin |
|---|---|
| On sale | Published, with the ticket link |
| Sale ended | Stays published, without the ticket link |
| Cancelled | **Avlyst** (Cancelled), and biso.no doesn't show it |
| Not on sale to the public, or sale paused | Not imported. An event imported earlier becomes a draft. |

An imported event has no department in **Hosted by**, and its address ends in a code from Tickster. Department members don't see imported events. A campus leader can set **Hosted by** to your department so you can see and edit it.

Each time the import runs, it writes these fields again from Tickster, in both languages:

- the title, the teaser and the description
- the start and end
- the venue address and the cover image
- the price and the ticket link
- whether it's part of a series
- the status: published, draft or cancelled

It also sets the event back to **Open to all BI students**. Change these things in Tickster, not in the admin. An edit you make in the admin is replaced at the next run. Saving the event as a draft doesn't take it off biso.no either: the next run publishes it again.

The import leaves these alone, so edit them in the admin:

- **Category**, **Tags** and **Hosted by**
- **Capacity**, **Member price** and **Registration closes**
- **Point of contact**
- the notification switch

Each new event the import brings in has its notification switched on. If it's published and not over, students who follow events for its campus get the automatic notification.

The import doesn't look for events made in the admin. If you also create the event yourself, it shows twice.

## What the system does on its own

- Sends one app notification when an event goes live, if its **Notify students when published** switch is on. The switch is on for every new event, for events the [BISO Assistant](/guide/start-here/everyday-tools#ask-the-biso-assistant-everyone) creates, and for events the Tickster import brings in. [How notifications reach students](/guide/announcements) explains who gets it and when.
- Takes an event off the biso.no list once it has ended, and closes RSVP.
- Imports and updates events from Tickster, when the import runs.
- Translates the other language in the background when **Auto-translate** is on.
- Writes creates, saves and deletions of events, and changes to logistics groups, to the activity log.

It never sends:

- an email about an event
- a reminder before an event starts
- a message when an event is changed, moved, unpublished or deleted
- a message when a student answers RSVP, to the student or to you
- a message when a student is placed in a group

## What you do yourself

- Publish the event. Nothing goes live by itself, and **Schedule for…** doesn't hold an event back.
- Add the ticket link, and set up sales and member prices with the ticket seller.
- Switch off **Notify students when published** before you publish an event that students shouldn't get a notification about.
- Tell students about changes. A campus leader or global admin can send an announcement linked to the event.
- Check RSVPs and import attendee lists on the logistics page, and put people in groups.
- Change imported events in Tickster.

The pages after this one show each task step by step: [Create an event](/guide/events/create-an-event) and [Run the event](/guide/events/run-the-event).
