<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- Only global admins and campus leaders have **Regnskap** (Accounting) in the sidebar. Anyone else who opens the page gets page not found.
- What you change here applies to the whole shop, on every campus. Agree changes with whoever keeps BISO's accounts before you make them.
- This page reads from 24SevenOffice (Finago) and decides what the shop posts there. You can't see 24SevenOffice from the admin, so the steps below are text only.
- Have the account numbers ready:
  - the voucher type the shop's vouchers should use
  - the clearing accounts for Vipps and for Stripe
  - a revenue account, from 3000 to 3999, for each kind of sale
- Every change on this page is written to the activity log.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the accounting page">

Choose **Regnskap** (Accounting) in the sidebar. The page is headed **Regnskap for nettbutikken** (Webshop accounting). It has four panels, from the top:

- **Automatisk bokføring** (Automatic posting): the switch that sends paid orders to 24SevenOffice.
- **Bilag** (Voucher): the voucher type and the clearing accounts.
- **Salgstyper** (Sales types): the kinds of sale products can be booked as.
- **Kontoplan** (Chart of accounts): the accounts copied from 24SevenOffice.

Work through them from the bottom up the first time.

</Step>
<Step title="Sync the chart of accounts">

In **Kontoplan** (Chart of accounts), choose **Synk kontoer fra Finago** (Sync accounts from Finago). It copies the accounts and their VAT codes from 24SevenOffice. When it's done, you see **Kontoplan synket** (Chart of accounts synced), and the panel shows **Sist synket** (Last synced) with the time. Before the first sync it says **Aldri synket** (Never synced).

If 24SevenOffice can't be reached, you see an error message, and the page keeps the accounts from the last sync.

Sync again whenever an account is added, closed or changed in 24SevenOffice. This page only knows the accounts and VAT codes from the last sync.

</Step>
<Step title="Set up sales types">

A sales type is a kind of sale, such as merchandise or locker rental. Each one books its sales to one revenue account. Product editors choose one for every product, and a product can't be published without it.

In **Salgstyper** (Sales types):

- If there are none yet, the panel says **Ingen salgstyper ennå.** (No sales types yet.) Choose **Opprett standard salgstyper** (Create default sales types) to add four:
  - **Egenandel (turer, hytteturer, arrangementer)** on account 3100
  - **Varesalg – klær og merch (25 % mva)** on account 3000
  - **Bokskapleie** on account 3620
  - **Annet avgiftsfritt salg** on account 3150

  Check that those accounts are right for BISO before you switch posting on.
- To add a sales type, fill in the empty last row: **Navn (norsk)** (Name (Norwegian)), **Navn (engelsk)** (Name (English)), **Inntektskonto** (Revenue account) and **Rekkefølge** (Order). Then choose **Legg til salgstype** (Add sales type).
- To change one, edit its row and choose **Lagre** (Save) on that row.

You see **Lagret** (Saved) when it's stored.

- **Inntektskonto** (Revenue account) (*Velg konto* (Choose account)) lists the active accounts from 3000 to 3999 in the last sync. An account that's missing from 24SevenOffice's copy, closed there, or has no VAT code yet can't be saved. Sync first.
- **Mva** (VAT) is read from the account. You don't type it. It shows **25 % mva (kode 3)** (25 % VAT (code 3)), **Avgiftsfritt (kode 5)** (VAT-exempt (code 5)), **Ingen mva (kode 0)** (No VAT (code 0)), another code, or **Ukjent — synk kontoer** (Unknown — sync accounts).
- **Rekkefølge** (Order) sets where the sales type comes in the lists.
- Both names are required, up to 80 characters each. Product editors see the Norwegian name and the account number.
- A new sales type gets a fixed id made from its Norwegian name. Renaming it later doesn't change the id. If the id is taken, you see **A sales type with this Norwegian label already exists**.
- To stop using a sales type, untick **Aktiv** (Active) and save. Products using it can't be published until they get another one. While a published product uses it, you see **1 live product uses this sales type — move it to another sales type before deactivating it.** Change that product's sales type first.

Each order line keeps the account, VAT code and department it was bought under. Changing a sales type, or a product's sales type, changes only orders placed after that.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the voucher and clearing accounts">

In **Bilag** (Voucher):

- **Bilagsart (nummer)** (Voucher type (number)): the voucher type the shop's vouchers use in 24SevenOffice. It's 8 until you change it.
- **Avregningskonto Vipps** (Vipps clearing account): 1530 until you change it.
- **Avregningskonto Stripe** (Stripe clearing account): 1540 until you change it.

Until you save, the panel says **Ikke lagret ennå — verdiene under er forslag** (Not saved yet — the values below are suggestions). Posting can't be switched on until you've saved, even if the suggestions are right.

Choose **Lagre** (Save). You see **Lagret** (Saved). Clearing accounts must be four-digit account numbers and the voucher type a positive number. Otherwise you see **Clearing accounts must be 4-digit account numbers and the voucher type a positive number**. While posting is on, each clearing account must also be in the last sync and active in 24SevenOffice, or you see a message starting **Sync accounts from Finago first**.

The panel explains how a sale is booked: the clearing account is debited with the gross amount, and the payout voucher from Vipps or Stripe credits it and books the fee.

Changing a clearing account doesn't move orders that are already posted. Refunds of earlier orders are booked against the new account.

</Step>
<Step title="Switch posting on">

**Automatisk bokføring** (Automatic posting) shows whether posting is on:

- **På — betalte ordre bokføres i Finago** (On — paid orders are posted to Finago)
- **Av — betalte ordre venter og bokføres når dette slås på** (Off — paid orders wait and are posted once this is switched on)

It's off until someone switches it on. Before you do, check:

- **Bilag** (Voucher) is saved.
- There's at least one sales type with **Aktiv** (Active) ticked, and no more than 100.
- Every active sales type's account, and both clearing accounts, are in the last sync and active in 24SevenOffice. Revenue accounts also need a VAT code.
- Paid orders that were refunded in part before posting was on are never posted automatically. Find them under **Bestillinger** (Orders): choose **Betalt** (Paid) and look for the **Delvis refundert** (Partially refunded) pill. Book their net sale by hand. See [Orders and refunds](/guide/shop/orders-and-refunds#what-happens-next).

Then choose **Slå på bokføring** (Switch posting on). You see **Lagret** (Saved). If something is missing, it stays off, and you see **Save the posting settings and add at least one active sales type before switching posting on**, or a message starting **Sync accounts from Finago first** that lists each account to fix.

To stop posting, choose **Slå av bokføring** (Switch posting off).

<Callout type="warn">
Switching posting on posts every paid shop order that's waiting, however old it is, a batch at a time. Switching it off again stops new postings, but vouchers already in 24SevenOffice stay there. Correct them in 24SevenOffice by hand.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step title="Check that orders are posted">

Open an order under **Bestillinger** (Orders). In **Bestillingsdetaljer** (Order details), **Regnskap** (Ledger) shows the voucher's transaction id once it's posted. [Orders and refunds](/guide/shop/orders-and-refunds#read-the-order) explains the other values.

In 24SevenOffice, a shop sale has the comment *Nettbutikk* followed by the order number, and a refund reversal *Refusjon nettbutikk* followed by the order number. Each voucher is dated the day it was posted, not the day of the order.

If a paid order isn't posted, check the sales type of each product in it, the product's department, and this page's settings. Once they're fixed, the order is tried again later. If **Regnskap** (Ledger) shows `posting` for more than a few minutes, ask IT.

</Step>
</Steps>

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- While posting is on, a paid shop order is posted to 24SevenOffice as soon as it's paid. An order that couldn't be posted is tried again by a scheduled check, a batch at a time.
- Each order is one voucher of the voucher type in **Bilag** (Voucher). The clearing account for Vipps or Stripe is debited with the total. Each line's revenue is credited to its sales type's account, with that account's VAT code and the product's department.
- Free orders, memberships and orders from the old WordPress shop aren't posted. A membership is booked as an invoice instead.
- Refunds of posted orders are reversed on their own. Other refunds need work by hand. See [Orders and refunds](/guide/shop/orders-and-refunds#what-happens-next).
- Nobody is notified when a posting succeeds or fails.
- Sales type changes, settings changes, the posting switch and each sync are written to the activity log.

<Callout type="warn">
Posting doesn't check whether a payment provider is in Test mode. A payment taken in Test mode is posted to 24SevenOffice like any other sale, and a refund of it is reversed like any other.
</Callout>
</WhatHappensNext>

## Common mistakes

- Switching posting on before the books are ready for every paid order that's waiting. It posts them all.
- Expecting **Slå av bokføring** (Switch posting off) to remove vouchers. It only stops new ones.
- Switching posting on under **System** → **Funksjonsbrytere** (Feature flags), where it's called *Webshop → Finago ledger posting*. That switch doesn't check that the settings are ready. Use **Slå på bokføring** (Switch posting on) on this page.
- Changing a setting for your own campus only. Everything on this page applies to every campus.
- Changing a clearing account and expecting posted orders to move. They stay where they were booked.
- Adding an account in 24SevenOffice and not syncing. It isn't in **Inntektskonto** (Revenue account) until you choose **Synk kontoer fra Finago** (Sync accounts from Finago).
- Typing a new name over an existing sales type to make a new one. That renames the existing one, and its products keep it. Use the empty last row to add a sales type.
- Unticking **Aktiv** (Active) on a sales type that published products use. It's refused. Move the products to another sales type first.
- Forgetting the partly refunded orders from before posting was switched on. Nothing reminds you to book them.
