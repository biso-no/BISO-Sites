<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- You've held the interviews and chosen who gets the role. See [Book interviews](/guide/recruitment/book-interviews).
- The offer is approved. **Offer** and **Archived** are final, and the student sees their new status on biso.no the moment you move them. See [Statuses](/guide/recruitment#statuses).
- The system sends no offer or rejection emails. You send each one from your own email, so plan to write it right after you move the candidate.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

The applications workspace is in English whatever your admin language, so this page names its labels in English.

<Steps>
<Step title="Move the chosen candidate to Offer">

Only a candidate in **Interview** can move to **Offer**. If the person you've chosen is in **Shortlist**, move them to **Interview** first.

1. On **Stillinger** (Jobs), choose the people icon on the vacancy's row. On a computer, the row's icons appear when you point at the row.
2. Choose the arrow icon on the candidate's card (tooltip: *Advance stage*), or **Advance** in their panel. The **Advance candidate** dialog opens.
3. From **Interview**, the dialog starts on **Shortlist**, which is a move back. In the row of statuses, choose **Offer**.
4. The box **Draft the offer email (only once HR has approved the offer)** is unticked. Tick it to get a draft. The dialog shows **Drafting with AI…**, then a subject and text you can edit.
5. Check and adjust the draft:
   - It's in Norwegian. The AI writes it from the candidate's name and the vacancy title only, so it has no other details about the role or the offer.
   - It tells the candidate they've been offered the role and suggests a follow-up call. Add what they need to know, such as when the role starts and how to reply.
   - The drafter is set to sign off with **Best regards, BISO HR**, in English. Change the sign-off to fit.
   - If you see **Failed to draft:** and a reason, write the email yourself.
6. Choose **Copy** to copy the subject and text. Do this before the next step. The dialog closes when the move is done, and the draft goes with it. Pressing Esc also closes the dialog and loses the draft.
7. Choose **Move to Offer**.

The candidate is now in the **Offer** column, and that's final. If you open **Advance** for them again, the dialog says **No further stage**.

You can also drag the card to the **Offer** column. That moves the candidate at once, with no dialog, no draft and no confirmation.

The **Suggested next steps** list in the dialog is a reminder. None of it happens by itself.

</Step>
<Step title="Send the offer yourself">

Paste the draft into a new email and send it to the candidate. Their email address is at the top of their panel. The system sends nothing.

Once the candidate is in **Offer**, their **Activity** list shows **Offer sent** and **Awaiting signature**, and the **Offers out** counter at the top says **Signature pending**. Those appear for everyone in **Offer**, whether or not you've emailed them. The admin doesn't track emails, replies or signatures, so keep track of the candidate's answer yourself.

</Step>
<Step title="Archive and write to one candidate">

Everyone you don't offer the role to goes to **Archived**, and each of them should hear from you. Use this step for a few candidates. For many, use the next step.

1. Choose the arrow icon on the candidate's card (tooltip: *Advance stage*), or **Advance** in their panel.
2. In the row of statuses, choose **Archived**.
3. **Draft a candidate notification email** is ticked. The draft is in Norwegian. It tells the candidate they weren't selected and thanks them for applying. Like the offer draft, it uses only their name and the vacancy title, so check and adjust it.
4. Choose **Copy**, then **Move to Archived**.
5. Paste the draft into an email and send it yourself.

If the candidate has an interview booked for a later time, archiving doesn't cancel it. The interview stays booked, and the calendar invite stays with the candidate and the panel. Tell them it's off.

</Step>
<Step title="Archive the rest at once">

With many candidates left, draft their emails first and then move them together. Keep to this order, because **Move stage** clears your ticks.

1. Tick the candidates. On the board, tick the box on each card. In the list (tooltip: *List*), the **Stage** column shows each candidate's status, and the box in the header ticks every candidate shown.
2. Untick anyone in **Offer** and anyone you've already written to. Otherwise they get a **Not this round** draft too.
3. Keep to 25 or fewer. Only the first 25 get a draft.
4. In the bar, choose **Archive**. This opens the drafts panel with **Not this round** selected. It doesn't archive anyone.
5. Each candidate shows **Drafted**, **Failed** or **…**. Click a name to read and edit their draft. Don't switch the type of email at the top after you've edited, because all the drafts are written again and your edits are lost.
6. Choose **Copy all drafts**. You get one block per candidate with **To:**, **Subject:** and the text. A failed draft gives a block with an empty subject and text. Delete those blocks and write those emails yourself. Send each block as its own email.
7. Close the drafts panel with the X. Your ticks are still there.
8. Open **Move stage** in the bar and choose **Archived**.

You then see one of these:

- A message such as **Successfully moved 6 candidates.** They're all in **Archived**.
- **Some candidates could not be moved to rejected.** A ticked candidate in **Offer** can't be archived. The others still move.
- A message such as **Moved 3/4 candidates. 1 failed.** The failed ones stay where they were, so try them again.

Repeat for the next group. The number on the **Pipeline** tab and **In pipeline** at the top count the candidates still in **Applied**, **Shortlist** or **Interview**. When they read 0, every candidate is in **Offer** or **Archived**.

To make several offers at once, use the same order with the candidates in **Interview** you've chosen. In the bar, choose **AI-personalize email ★**, then choose **Offer letter** at the top of the drafts panel. Copy the drafts, then use **Move stage** and choose **Offer**.

</Step>
<Step title="End the round">

The admin has no button that closes a vacancy. The deadline ends the round.

- **The vacancy has a deadline.** You don't need to do anything. At the end of the deadline day, Oslo time, the vacancy leaves biso.no and the app, and nobody else can apply. **Stillinger** (Jobs) still shows it as **Publisert** (Published), with **Frist passert** (Past deadline).
- **It has no deadline, or you want to stop applications now.** Open the vacancy with the pencil icon on its row in **Stillinger** (Jobs), or with **Edit posting** in the workspace. Choose **Save Draft**. The vacancy goes offline at once, without asking you to confirm. You see **Stilling lagret** (Job saved successfully), and the list shows **Utkast** (Draft). On a scheduled vacancy that isn't live yet, **Save Draft** also cancels the schedule.

To stop applications at the end of a chosen day instead, set an earlier **Application deadline** on the **Logistics** step and save with **Publish**.

Ending the round doesn't change or delete any application. You can still open the applications with the people icon, and each application is deleted on its own date. See [What happens next](#what-happens-next).

</Step>
</Steps>

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- The student sees their new status on biso.no as soon as you move them. Nothing else tells them. They get only the emails you send.
- Nobody can move a candidate out of **Offer** or **Archived**. The admin refuses the move for everyone, global admins included.
- Interviews you've booked stay booked, with their calendar invites. The admin has no way to cancel them.
- At the deadline, or when you choose **Save Draft**, the vacancy leaves biso.no and the app, and new applications are refused. After **Save Draft**, the workspace header shows **draft** where it said **Live**.
- Each application has a delete date, set when the student applied. It's 180 days after the vacancy's deadline, or 180 days after the student applied if the vacancy had no deadline. Taking the vacancy offline or changing the deadline later doesn't move the date.
- After that date, the system deletes the application with its CV, cover letter, answers and interviews.
- For the next round, copy this vacancy with the copy icon on its row (tooltip: *Dupliser for en ny runde*). See [Publish a vacancy](/guide/recruitment/publish-a-vacancy).
</WhatHappensNext>

## What the student sees

On biso.no, on **Mine søknader** (My applications):

| You move them to | They see |
|---|---|
| **Offer** | **Tilbud** (Accepted) |
| **Archived** | **Ikke valgt** (Not selected) |

- The label changes as soon as you move them, with no email or notification. A student you archive can see **Ikke valgt** (Not selected) before your email arrives, so send it right away.
- The application stays on the page, with **Lagres til** (Stored until) and its delete date. Once the application is deleted, it's no longer listed.
- **Se vervet** (View vacancy) on the application opens the vacancy's page. Once the deadline has passed or you've taken the vacancy offline, that page shows page not found.
- An interview booked for a later time still shows under **Kommende intervju** (Upcoming interview), even after you archive the candidate.

## Common mistakes

- Forgetting the people who weren't selected. They see **Ikke valgt** (Not selected) and get no email unless you send one. Write to everyone you archive, including people you archived early in the round.
- Moving someone to **Offer** or **Archived** before you're sure. Both are final, and the admin has no undo. Dropping a card on either column moves the candidate without asking. If you move the wrong person, write to them straight away, because they already see the new status.
- Using the bulk **Archive** button to archive. It only drafts **Not this round** emails, and everyone stays where they were. To archive several candidates, use **Move stage** and choose **Archived**.
- Choosing **Move to Shortlist** for the person you want to hire. From **Interview**, the dialog starts on **Shortlist**. Choose **Offer** in the row of statuses first.
- Leaving the person you're hiring ticked with the rest. They get a **Not this round** draft too. Untick anyone in **Offer** before you choose **Archive**.
- Choosing **Move to**, or pressing Esc, before you copy the draft. The dialog closes and the draft is lost. To draft again, tick the candidate, choose **AI-personalize email ★** in the bar and pick the type of email.
- Reading **Offer sent** or **Signature pending** as a sign that something went out. They show for everyone in **Offer**. The system sends no offers.
- Waiting for the vacancy to close by itself when it has no deadline. It stays on biso.no and in the app, taking applications, until you choose **Save Draft**.
- Archiving a candidate who still has an interview booked, and not telling them. The interview and the invites stay. Tell the candidate and the panel.
