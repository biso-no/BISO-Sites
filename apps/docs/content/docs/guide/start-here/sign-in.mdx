<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- You need a BISO Microsoft account.
- The account must be in the BISO group for your campus and the group for your department. These groups give you your role. Benefits managers get their access from a separate list instead.
- If you don't have an account, or you're missing a group, ask IT. In the admin, only global admins can create accounts and change groups.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Sign in">

Open the BISO admin in your browser. If you aren't signed in, you see a page headed **Velkommen tilbake** (Welcome Back). The sign-in page is always in Norwegian.

Choose **Logg inn med BISO-konto** (Sign in with BISO account), then sign in to Microsoft with your BISO account. There is no other way to sign in.

When Microsoft sends you back, the admin reads your BISO groups before it opens. That is how it knows your role.

If the sign-in page shows **Innlogging mislyktes. Prøv igjen eller kontakt support.** (Sign-in failed. Please try again or contact support.), choose **Logg inn med BISO-konto** (Sign in with BISO account) again.

</Step>
<Step title="See where you land">

Where you land depends on your role:

- Global admins and campus admins land on **Oversikt** (Overview), the dashboard.
- Everyone else lands on the first item in their sidebar. For HR and department members that is **Sider** (Pages). To get to recruitment, open **Innhold** (Content) and choose **Stillinger** (Jobs).

Near the top of the sidebar, a box shows your campus with your role under it. For HR and department members, the role is your department's name, for example **HR**. Campus admins see **Campusadministrator** (Campus Admin) and their campus. Global admins see **Global administrator** (Global Admin), and benefits managers see **Fordelsansvarlig** (Benefits manager).

Global admins can click the campus box to show one campus or all of them. For everyone else the box only shows your own campus.

The bottom of the sidebar shows **User** instead of your name, then your role and the sign-out icon.

</Step>
<Step title="Find your way in the sidebar">

The sidebar shows only what your role can open. Groups such as **Innhold** (Content) open and close when you click them. The group that holds the page you're on opens by itself.

| Your role | What the sidebar shows |
|---|---|
| Department member | **Innhold** (Content) with **Sider** (Pages), **Nyheter** (News) and **Arrangementer** (Events). **Produkter** (Products). **Organisasjon** (Organization) with **Avdelinger** (Departments) and **Dokumenter** (Documents). |
| HR or National HR | The same, plus **Stillinger** (Jobs) under **Innhold** (Content). |
| Campus admin | **Oversikt** (Overview), **Innboks** (Inbox) and **Medlemmer** (Members). Under **Innhold** (Content), the department items plus **Kommunikasjon** (Communications), **Fordeler** (Benefits) and **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts). **Produkter** (Products), **Regnskap** (Accounting) and **Organisasjon** (Organization). **System** with **IT** and **Aktivitet** (Activity). |
| Global admin | Everything, including **Stillinger** (Jobs), **Analyse** (Analytics) and the whole **System** group. |
| Benefits manager | **Fordeler** (Benefits), plus anything your department gives you. |

Only HR and global admins see **Stillinger** (Jobs).

To jump straight to a page, press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on other computers. You can also click **Hurtigsøk i admin...** (Quick search in admin...) in the top bar. Quick search is in English. It lists the pages your role can open. When you type two or more letters, it also searches jobs and other content.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the language">

The admin is in Norwegian unless your account is set to English. This guide names each label in Norwegian, with the English in brackets.

In the admin, only global admins can change the language, and only their own. They open **System**, then **Innstillinger** (Settings), pick **English** or **Norwegian** under **Standardspråk** (Default Locale), and choose **Lagre innstillinger** (Save Preferences).

Some screens are in English whatever your language. In recruitment, the vacancy editor and the applications workspace are in English.

</Step>
<Step title="Open the notification bell">

The bell at the top right opens **Notifications**. The panel is in English.

It lists active notices. Choosing a notice marks it as read and opens its link, if it has one. **Mark all read** marks them all. The admin remembers what you've read only in this browser, so on another computer the notices show as unread again.

Global admins and campus admins see two tabs, **Notices** and **Pending**. **Pending** lists draft jobs, events and news.

The bell never tells you about new job applications. To see them, open the vacancy from **Stillinger** (Jobs).

</Step>
<Step title="Take the recruitment tour (HR and global admins)">

When you open **Stillinger** (Jobs), a short tour starts by itself. It shows your campus, the vacancy list, the **Utlys stilling** (Post Job) button and the vacancy editor. If you can see at least one vacancy, the tour also opens the applications for the first vacancy in your list. There it shows the **Pipeline**, **Interviews**, **Funnel & sources**, **Form & rubric** and **Access & data** tabs. The last step plays a guide video.

Move through it with **Neste** (Next) and **Tilbake** (Back). On the last step, choose **Ferdig** (Done). **Hopp over** (Skip tour), or the X next to the step counter, ends the tour early.

The tour keeps starting each time you open **Stillinger** (Jobs) until you finish or skip it. After that it doesn't start again, on any computer, because the admin remembers this for your account. To watch the video again, choose **Se videoguiden** (Watch the guide) on the **Stillinger** (Jobs) page.

</Step>
<Step title="Sign out">

Choose the sign-out icon at the bottom right of the sidebar, next to your role. You go back to the sign-in page. You can also choose **Sign Out** in quick search.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Common mistakes

- Signing in with a personal Microsoft account. The admin gives you access from the BISO groups of the account you sign in with. A personal account isn't in those groups, so it gets no access. Use your BISO account.
- Being shown **Du må være logget inn for å se denne siden** (You must be signed in to view this page) right after you signed in. You are signed in, but your account isn't in a BISO campus or department group yet. Ask IT to add you. That page has no sign-out button, and **Logg inn** (Sign in) brings you straight back to it. Once IT has added you, open the admin in a private browser window and sign in there. After that, the admin also works in your normal window when you reload it.
- Waiting for a new role to show up. The admin reads your groups only when you sign in. If IT changes your groups while you're signed in, sign out and sign in again.
- Expecting a link to open after you sign in. If you open a link to an admin page while signed out, you land on your start page after sign-in. Open the link again.
- Using a phone. On a narrow screen the sidebar and the quick search bar are hidden. Use a computer, or make the browser window wider.
