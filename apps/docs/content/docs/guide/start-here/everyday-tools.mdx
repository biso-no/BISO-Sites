<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- You're signed in to the admin. [Sign in and find your way](/guide/start-here/sign-in) covers signing in, the sidebar, the language setting and the notification bell.
- Each step below says who sees the tool. Department members, HR and benefits editors have quick search and the assistant. The other tools aren't in their sidebar.
- Quick search and the assistant panel are in English whatever your admin language, so this page gives their labels in English.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Read the overview (global admins and campus leaders)">

Global admins and campus leaders land on **Oversikt** (Overview) when they sign in. Everyone else goes to the first item in their sidebar.

The heading reads **God dag, Admin** (Good day, Admin). It says "Admin" for everyone. The header has two buttons:

- **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) opens the drafts list. See the next step.
- **Opprett side** (Create Page) opens **Sider** (Pages).

Below the heading are four counts. Each one links to its area and is marked **Åpne arbeidsområde** (Open workspace).

| Count | What it counts | Where it links |
|---|---|---|
| **Åpne stillinger** (Open Jobs) | Published jobs | **Stillinger** (Jobs), which only global admins and HR can open |
| **Kommende arrangementer** (Upcoming Events) | Every published event, past ones included | **Arrangementer** (Events) |
| **Publiserte artikler** (Published Articles) | Published news | **Nyheter** (News) |
| **Ventende utkast** (Pending Drafts) | Draft jobs only | **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) |

The counts follow your campus. Campus leaders see their own campus. Global admins see all campuses, or the one they picked in the campus box at the top of the sidebar. A count that can't be loaded shows 0, with no error message.

The **Activity** chart covers the **Last 14 days**, with **Events**, **News** and **Jobs** shown separately. It counts each day's entries in the activity log, such as an item being created or edited. It doesn't show how many items were published. It reads only the latest 200 log entries, so a busy fortnight is cut short.

Under the chart, **Nylig aktivitet** (Recent Activity) lists the five latest entries in the activity log. **Se all aktivitet** (View all activity) opens the full log. The chart and this list include every campus.

The overview doesn't show the inbox. New form submissions and waiting requests are counted in the red number on **Innboks** (Inbox) in the sidebar. See [Inbox and approvals](/guide/start-here/inbox-and-approvals).

</Step>
<Step title="Review drafts (global admins and campus leaders)">

Open **Innhold** (Content) and choose **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts), or choose **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) on the overview. The heading shows how many are waiting, for example **4 ventende** (4 pending).

The list holds draft jobs, events and news for your campus. Global admins see all campuses, or the one they picked in the campus box. The list takes up to 50 of each type, most recently edited first. Pages, products, benefits and documents never appear here.

Each card shows:

- the cover image, for events and news
- the type: **Stilling** (Job), **Arrangement** (Event) or **Artikkel** (Article)
- the Norwegian title, or **Untitled job**, **Untitled event** or **Untitled article** when there isn't one
- the date it was last edited

To check a draft, choose the link icon at the bottom of the card (tooltip: *Forhåndsvis* (Preview)). It opens the item's editor. There's no preview of the public page on this screen.

To publish the draft, choose **Godkjenn** (Approve). It goes live at once, with no confirmation. You see **Utkast godkjent og publisert** (Draft approved and published), and the card leaves the list.

If a draft isn't ready, leave it. It stays a draft, and stays in this list, until someone approves it or publishes it from its editor.

Campus leaders also see draft jobs for their campus here. **Stillinger** (Jobs) is only for HR and global admins, so for campus leaders the link icon on a job opens a page that isn't found. Leave job drafts to HR and the global admins.

The notification bell's **Pending** tab also lists draft jobs, events and news. See [Sign in and find your way](/guide/start-here/sign-in#open-the-notification-bell).

<Callout type="warn">
**Godkjenn** (Approve) publishes the item on biso.no straight away. Open the draft with the link icon and check it first.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step title="Check the activity log (global admins and campus leaders)">

Open **System** and choose **Aktivitet** (Activity), or choose **Se all aktivitet** (View all activity) on the overview. The page is headed **Aktivitetslogg** (Activity Log).

The log lists actions taken in the admin, newest first. Each row shows:

- who did it: their email, or **System** for something that ran by itself, such as a vacancy going live at its scheduled time
- what they did, as a code name, for example `event.create`, `page_published` or `draft_approved`
- the type of item, with the first eight characters of its id
- the date and time

Change how many rows you see with **Per side** (Per page) at the bottom.

The log covers every campus. Campus leaders see entries from all campuses, and the campus box doesn't change what a global admin sees here.

You can't search, filter or export the log, and you can't open a row for more detail.

Not everything is logged. Changes to form submissions aren't, for example. If an entry can't be saved, the action still goes through and the log misses it.

</Step>
<Step title="Find things with quick search (everyone)">

Open quick search in any of these ways:

- Press ⌘K on a Mac or Ctrl+K on other computers.
- Click **Hurtigsøk i admin...** (Quick search in admin...) in the top bar.
- Click the ⌘ K button next to **BISO Studio** at the top of the sidebar.

Move with the arrow keys and press Enter to open what's highlighted. Esc closes quick search.

With nothing typed, you see:

- **Recent**: the last five admin pages and items you opened. They're kept in this browser, so another computer has its own list.
- **Navigate**: the admin pages your role can open. A few aren't here: **Medlemmer** (Members), **Regnskap** (Accounting), **Varsling** (Whistleblowing) and **Appversjoner** (App versions). Use the sidebar for those. Some names differ from the sidebar: **Shop** is **Produkter** (Products), **Drafts** is **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) and **IT Console** is **IT**.
- **Create**: new items in the areas your role can open. That's **New Event** and **New News Article**, plus **New Job Posting** for HR and global admins. Each opens the editor for a new item.
- **Account**: **Sign Out**.

Global admins also get **Switch campus: Oslo**, **Switch campus: Bergen**, **Switch campus: Trondheim**, **Switch campus: Stavanger** and **Switch campus: All campuses**. They do the same as the campus box in the sidebar.

Type to narrow the list. Once you've typed two letters, quick search also looks through content. Results come in groups: **Jobs**, **Events**, **News**, **Pages**, **Departments**, **Products** and **Orders**, with up to five in each. It matches titles, department names, and for orders the buyer's name or email. Each result shows its status, or its campus for a department. Choose one to open it.

You only get results your role and campus let you open. Orders, for example, show only for global admins and campus leaders.

While it searches, you see **Searching…**. If the search fails, you see **Search unavailable**. Try again in a moment.

Quick search doesn't find members, form submissions, approval requests, documents or benefits, and it doesn't search the text of news articles.

When the assistant is on, the last group, **AI**, has one row: **Ask BISO Assistant:** followed by what you typed, or **Open BISO Assistant** if you typed nothing. Choosing it opens the assistant and sends your text as the first message.

</Step>
<Step title="Ask the BISO Assistant (everyone)">

Open the assistant in any of these ways:

- Choose **Spør BISO Assistant** (Ask BISO Assistant) near the bottom of the sidebar.
- Choose the **AI** row in quick search.
- On a narrow screen, tap the round sparkle button at the bottom right.

A panel titled **BISO Assistant** slides in from the right. Close it with the X in its header. The panel is in English whatever your admin language.

An empty panel shows **What can I help with?** and a few suggestions for your role. Choose a suggestion to send it, or type in **Ask BISO Assistant…**. Press Enter to send, and Shift+Enter for a new line. A message can be up to 2,000 characters.

What the assistant can do depends on your role. It can search and read the content you have access to, and take you to pages. Global admins and campus leaders get more, such as approving requests.

When it opens a page for you, the admin goes there straight away, and the chat shows a note of where it went. The conversation stays while you move around the admin. If you reload or close the page, it's gone.

In the job editor and the event editor, the assistant can fill in the form on your screen. It doesn't save. Check the fields, then save yourself.

Before it creates, changes, publishes or deletes anything, the assistant is set up to show you a card and wait for your choice:

- New content gets a draft card with **NO** and **EN** tabs. **Edit** lets you change the title and text. **Approve & save** goes ahead, and the X drops it.
- Other changes get a card with **Confirm**, or a red **Delete** for a deletion, and **Cancel**.

Read each card before you choose.

If you ask it to publish something your role can't publish on that campus, the item stays a draft, or is saved as one, and the assistant shows an **Approval required** card. Choose **Send approval request** to send a request, or the X to drop it. The request goes to the inbox of the campus leaders for the item's campus. Requests for jobs, and for National or no campus, go to the global admins. Global admins can see every request.

Nobody is told about the request, and you aren't told when it's decided. When it's approved, the item is published, so check the item in its own list. If it's rejected, the item stays as it was, and nothing in the admin tells you why. Ask the approvers. See [Inbox and approvals](/guide/start-here/inbox-and-approvals).

<Callout type="warn">
**Delete** on an assistant card deletes the item for good. There's no undo.
</Callout>

If **Spør BISO Assistant** (Ask BISO Assistant) opens nothing and quick search has no **AI** row, the assistant is switched off for the whole admin. Ask the global admins.

</Step>
</Steps>

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- None of these tools sends an email, a push or a Teams message of its own.
- Approving a draft publishes it on biso.no at once. Events and news show on the biso.no lists, and jobs on biso.no/jobs. For an event, news item or job, its automatic push notification may follow, depending on the item's own notification setting.
- Approving a draft is written to the activity log with your email.
- An approval request sent from the assistant waits in the approvers' inbox. Nobody gets a message about it.
</WhatHappensNext>

## What the student sees

Students and members never see these tools. They see what comes out of them: a draft you approve shows on biso.no like any other published item.

## Common mistakes

- Reading **Ventende utkast** (Pending Drafts) on the overview as all drafts. It counts draft jobs only. Open **Gjennomgå utkast** (Review Drafts) to see draft events and news too.
- Reading **Kommende arrangementer** (Upcoming Events) as events still to come. It counts every published event, past ones included.
- Trusting a 0 on the overview. A count that couldn't be loaded shows 0. Reload the page if a number looks wrong.
- Approving a draft to see how it looks. **Godkjenn** (Approve) publishes it at once. Open it with the link icon instead. To take a published item offline again, unpublish it in its own editor.
- Expecting the activity log to show only your campus. It shows actions on every campus.
- Losing an assistant conversation. Reloading or closing the admin clears it. Copy anything you need first.
- Choosing **Confirm** or **Delete** on an assistant card without reading it. A deletion can't be undone.
- Looking for a member or a form submission in quick search. Use **Medlemmer** (Members) or **Innboks** (Inbox).
