This guide is for the people who run BISO through the admin: HR, department editors, campus leaders, benefits editors and IT. It is organised by area of work. Most areas open with a page on how the area works, followed by one page for each task. Analytics is a single page.

Not sure where to start? [Pick your role](/guide/start-here/pick-your-role) and follow the list for it. Everyone can begin with [Sign in and find your way](/guide/start-here/sign-in).

## The areas

- [Start here](/guide/start-here/pick-your-role): signing in, everyday tools, the inbox and approvals.
- [Recruitment](/guide/recruitment): publish a vacancy, review applications, book interviews and make offers.
- [Pages and news](/guide/pages-and-news): edit pages and publish news on biso.no.
- [Departments and documents](/guide/departments-and-documents): department pages and the document library.
- [Events](/guide/events): create an event and run it.
- [Announcements](/guide/announcements): send a message to students as a notification.
- [Shop](/guide/shop): products, orders, refunds and accounting.
- [Members and benefits](/guide/members-and-benefits): find members, publish benefits and scan passes.
- [Access and settings](/guide/access-and-settings): users, role accounts and system settings.
- [Analytics](/guide/analytics): read the website figures.
