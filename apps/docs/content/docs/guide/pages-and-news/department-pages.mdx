<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- Most departments have a standard page on biso.no, at biso.no/units/ followed by its campus and its own short name. For example, Demo Events in Oslo would be at biso.no/units/oslo/demo-events. biso.no builds the standard page from the department's details in the admin.
- Accounting units and national bodies, such as the national board, national HR and the Control Committee, have no page on biso.no. A page made for one of them isn't shown.
- You can build the department its own page in the page editor instead. Once that page is published in a language, visitors who read biso.no in that language see it in place of the standard page, at the same address.
- A project, such as Fadderullan or Karrieredagene, is a department too. Its page works the same way.
- Who can do what:
  - Department members can create and edit their own department's page.
  - Campus leaders can do it for any department on their campus.
  - Global admins can do it for every department.
- You're a member of a department through your BISO Microsoft account. If you can't open your department, ask IT to check your groups.
- The page editor is in English. [Edit a page](/guide/pages-and-news/edit-a-page) shows how it works.
</BeforeYouStart>

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your department">

Open **Organisasjon** (Organization) and choose **Avdelinger** (Departments).

- If you're a member of one department, it opens straight away.
- If you're in several, or you're a campus leader or global admin, choose the department in the list.

The department opens with its name at the top and its campus under it. The **Offentlig side** (Public page) card is about its page on biso.no.

If you're on the board of a national project, such as Winter Games, open its page from here. It isn't in your **Sider** (Pages) list.

</Step>
<Step title="Create the page">

If the department has no page yet, the card reads **Ingen side ennå. Opprett én for å gi avdelingen sin egen side på biso.no.** (No page yet. Create one to give this department its own page on biso.no.)

Choose **Opprett side** (Create page). The page is made as a draft and opens in the page editor. If your admin is in English, the editor opens on **EN**, which is empty. Choose **NO** to see the starting page. It doesn't start blank:

- a page header with the department's name, and BISO and the campus above it
- **Om** and the department's name: a text block for what you do
- **Kommende arrangementer**: an events feed that shows the department's own published events
- **Ta kontakt**: a contact block

A project's page also gets an empty gallery and a **For bedrifter** text block for companies. Change or remove any of it.

The starting page is in Norwegian only. You write or translate the English yourself.

Once the page exists, the card shows **Rediger side** (Edit Page) instead. A department never has two pages.

Instead of the button, you may see:

- **Denne avdelingen er inaktiv, så det kan ikke opprettes en side for den.** (This department is inactive, so a page cannot be created for it.) An inactive department has no page on biso.no.
- **Denne avdelingen har ingen slug ennå. Kjør avdelingssynkroniseringen for å tildele en.** (This department has no slug yet. Run the department sync to assign one.) Only global admins can run the sync, so ask them.
- **Denne adressen er allerede i bruk av en annen side. Ta kontakt med BISO IT for å løse dette før en side kan opprettes her.** (This address is already used by another page. Contact BISO IT to resolve it before a page can be created here.) Contact IT.

</Step>
<Step title="Edit the page">

Build the page as in [Edit a page](/guide/pages-and-news/edit-a-page). There are a few differences:

- The address, campus and department are fixed. On the **Page** tab, **Shared slug**, **Campus** and **Dept** can't be changed, and a note says *Managed by the department — this page is published at its unit URL.*
- The **Theme** colours are the department's. When you publish, they apply to all of the department's pages, and to its standard page on biso.no.
- Live blocks such as **Events feed**, **Open roles** and **News feed** follow the department, under **This page's department**.

To come back to the page later, choose **Rediger side** (Edit Page) on the **Offentlig side** (Public page) card.

</Step>
<Step title="Publish each language">

In the editor, choose **Publish** with **NO** selected. Then choose **EN**, write the English page or use **Translate to EN**, and choose **Publish changes**. Once one language is live, the button has that name in both languages, even in one that has never been published.

Back on the department's page in the admin, the **Offentlig side** (Public page) card shows the page's address and one line for each language, **Norsk** (Norwegian) and **Engelsk** (English):

| Shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Publisert** (Published) | That language is live on biso.no. |
| **Utkast** (Draft) | That language has a draft, but isn't live. A language you have unpublished shows this too. |
| **Ikke publisert** (Not published) | That language has never been written. |

**Se publisert side** (View live) opens the page on biso.no. It shows once at least one language is live, if the department is active.

</Step>
<Step title="Go back to the standard page">

To show the standard page again, first look at the **Offentlig side** (Public page) card to see which languages read **Publisert** (Published). Open the page with **Rediger side** (Edit Page) and choose **Unpublish** in each of those languages. **Unpublish** shows in both languages, but it does nothing in a language that isn't live. Then check that the card reads **Utkast** (Draft) for both.

The standard page comes back at the same address, in each language you unpublish. Your page stays as a draft, so you can publish it again later.

</Step>
<Step title="Make a project's shared page (global admins)">

Some projects run on several campuses: Fadderullan, Karrieredagene and Bergensbaneløpet. Each campus's project has its own department page. The project as a whole can also have a shared page at biso.no/projects/ followed by the project's name, such as biso.no/projects/fadderullan.

On **Sider** (Pages), choose the project in the list beside **Opprett prosjektside** (Create project page), then choose **Opprett prosjektside**. The page opens in the editor, starting from the same layout as a project's department page. It belongs to no department, and only global admins can edit, publish or delete it.

Winter Games is one national project, so the button makes the page of its national department instead. Its board edits it from **Avdelinger** (Departments), as above. If that department isn't set up, you see **The Winter Games unit isn't set up yet**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- Creating the page saves a draft. Nothing changes on biso.no, and nobody is told.
- When you publish a language, your page replaces the standard page in that language.
- The colours you publish become the department's colours.
- No email or notification goes out when a department page is created or published.
- The department's address never changes, even when the department is renamed. Links to it keep working.
</WhatHappensNext>

## What the student sees

- At biso.no/units/ followed by the campus and the department's short name, a visitor sees your page when it's published in their language and has something on it: a page header or at least one section. Otherwise they see the standard page.
- biso.no/units/ followed by the department's short name alone, with no campus, uses the campus the visitor has chosen on biso.no. If that doesn't settle it, they're asked to pick a campus.
- An inactive department has no page on biso.no, even if its page is published.
- The department's colours show on its page and on its standard page.
- For Winter Games, the department's address sends visitors to biso.no/projects/winter-games once that page is published.
- At biso.no/projects/ followed by a project's name, visitors see the project's shared page once it's published in their language. Until then, biso.no shows its built-in project page, where the project has one.

## Common mistakes

- Publishing only Norwegian. The page starts in Norwegian only, so visitors who read biso.no in English keep seeing the standard page until you publish English too.
- Deleting the page from **Sider** (Pages) with the bin icon (**Slett side**, Delete page) that shows when you point at its row. The page is archived and the standard page comes back, but the **Offentlig side** (Public page) card can still show **Publisert** (Published). To hide your page, use **Unpublish** instead. To bring an archived page back, open it with **Rediger side** (Edit Page) and publish each language.
- Emptying the page. A published page with no header and no sections counts as no page, so biso.no shows the standard page.
- Changing the colours without meaning to change them everywhere. Publishing changes the colours of every page the department has, and of its standard page.
- Trying to change the page's address. It's tied to the department and can't be changed.
- Looking for a national project's page in **Sider** (Pages). Open it from **Avdelinger** (Departments).
