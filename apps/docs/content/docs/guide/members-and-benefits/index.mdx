This area of the admin covers BISO's paying members and what they get. It has two parts:

- **Medlemmer** (Members), in the sidebar. It holds the members list and the tools for scanning member passes at the door.
- **Fordeler** (Benefits), under **Innhold** (Content). It holds the benefits board, the partners behind the benefits, the partner logos on biso.no and the texts that say what membership gives.

Students buy membership on biso.no. The plans and prices come from 24SevenOffice, and there's no screen in the admin to change them. See [Membership isn't a shop product](/guide/shop#membership-isnt-a-shop-product).

## Who can use members and benefits

| Who | Members list | Scanning, guest links and scanner access | Benefits, partners and logos | Membership texts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global admins (National + Operations Unit) | Every campus, and **Oppdater nå** (Refresh now) | Yes, for any campus or all campuses | Every campus and **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) | Yes |
| Campus leaders (Ledelsen on your campus) | Members on your campus | You can scan any member's pass. Guest links and scanner access for your campus | Your campus. **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) is read-only | No |
| Benefits editors | No | No | Every campus and **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) | Yes |
| Department members, HR and everyone else | No | No | No | No |

- **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) holds the benefits, partners and logos that apply on every campus.
- The benefits editor role doesn't come from your Microsoft groups. IT keeps a list of benefits editors. To get the role, ask IT to add you to the benefits editor list, then sign in with your BISO Microsoft account as usual. See [Sign in and find your way](/guide/start-here/sign-in).
- A benefits editor needs no campus or department group. Their sidebar shows only **Fordeler** (Benefits), and the admin opens there. The role under their name reads **Fordelsansvarlig** (Benefits manager).
- A campus leader who is also on the benefits editor list gets a benefits editor's rights in **Fordeler** (Benefits). The role under their name still reads **Campusadministrator** (Campus Admin).
- Global admins see the members on every campus, or only the campus they picked in the campus box.
- A member whose campus isn't known is only in the list for global admins.
- Only people who can edit a benefit's campus see its discount code or link. A campus leader who opens a **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) benefit sees it read-only, with no code.
- Opening a page your role can't use shows a page-not-found error.

If you think you should have access and don't, ask IT to check your groups.

## The members list

**Medlemmer** (Members) lists the paid BISO members from 24SevenOffice: name, email, campus, plan and expiry date. It's a copy. A sync copies the members from 24SevenOffice, and a global admin can start one at any time with **Oppdater nå** (Refresh now).

Scanning and member benefits don't use the list. Each scan, and each time a member asks for a code, checks the membership with 24SevenOffice. So a new member's pass can work before they show up in the list. See [Find a member](/guide/members-and-benefits/find-a-member).

## Member passes and scanning

Every member has a member pass. It's in the BISO app as **Medlemskort** (Member pass), and on biso.no in the member portal. The pass on biso.no shows a code that changes every 30 seconds. A pass can also be in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

The pass in the app and on biso.no shows the day's colour, such as **Blå** (Blue), and so does each scanner. A colour is picked for each day, so a guard can compare the pass with the scanner and spot a screenshot from another day. Wallet passes don't show the colour.

There are three ways to scan:

| Way | For | How they get in |
|---|---|---|
| **Skann medlemskort** (Scan passes) in the admin | Global admins and campus leaders | Their own admin sign-in |
| A guest scanner link | Guards at an event who have no BISO account | A link you make. It works in a phone's browser for up to 48 hours |
| Scanner access in the BISO app | People outside BISO who scan in the BISO app | They sign in to the app with the email address you gave access to |

All three give the same answers. Each scan checks the code on the pass, then asks 24SevenOffice whether the person is a member right now. See [Scan member passes](/guide/members-and-benefits/scan-member-passes).

## Benefits and their status

The board under **Fordeler** (Benefits) has five sections, in this order:

- **Medlemsfordeler** (Member benefits): discounts and offers from partners. Each one says how students get it: show the member pass, a discount code, a link, or nothing.
- **Karrierefordeler** (Career advantages), **Sosialt nettverk** (Social network), **Trygghet og støtte** (Safety & support) and **For bedrifter** (For companies). These are one line each.

Each benefit has a pill that shows its status. The status comes from the button you last used, the dates and the partner:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Utkast** (Draft) | Saved with **Lagre utkast** (Save draft). Not shown to students. |
| **Synlig** (Visible) | Published and live. Shown on biso.no and in the app. |
| **Planlagt** (Scheduled) | Published, with a start date still to come. It goes live by itself at midnight on the start date, Norwegian time. |
| **Utløpt** (Expired) | Published, but its end date has passed. It came down by itself at the end of that day. |
| **Avsluttet** (Ended) | Published, but the partnership with its partner has ended. |
| **Skjult** (Hidden) | Taken down with **Skjul fra nettsiden** (Hide from the website). |

Only **Synlig** (Visible) benefits are shown to students. See [Publish a benefit](/guide/members-and-benefits/publish-a-benefit) and [Partners and membership texts](/guide/members-and-benefits/partners-and-texts).

## What's members-only

- A visible benefit is public. Anyone, signed in or not, can see its title, description, partner, image and terms on biso.no and in the app.
- Only the discount code or link is for members. It's stored apart from the benefit and handed out only to an active member, after their membership is checked.
- There's no switch that makes a whole benefit members-only. Anything you write in the title, description or terms is public.

## What members see

On biso.no:

- **biso.no/membership** lists the member benefits, grouped by category when there's more than one. The other four sections show there as short lists of titles. Campus pages and biso.no/students show benefits too.
- A visitor who has picked a campus sees that campus's benefits first, then **Hele BISO** (All of BISO). With no campus picked, they see **Hele BISO** (All of BISO) only. Within each group, the order is the one on the board.
- In the member portal, biso.no/member, the **Fordeler** (Benefits) tab lists the member benefits for the member's campus. A benefit with a code or link has a **Vis kode** (Reveal Code) button. One where students show their pass says **Vis medlemskortet i appen** (Show your member card in the app).
- A student who isn't a member and chooses **Vis kode** (Reveal Code) gets **Du trenger et aktivt medlemskap for å se denne fordelen. Bli medlem i BISO for å låse den opp.** (You need an active membership to see this benefit. Join BISO to unlock it.)

In the BISO app:

- Benefits are listed for the campus. A benefit opens with its partner, title and description, its end date and terms if it has them, and one button: **Vis kode** (Show code), **Åpne lenken** (Open the link) or **Vis medlemskortet i kassa** (Show your membership card at the checkout).
- A student who isn't signed in sees **Logg inn for å se koden** (Sign in to see the code). One who isn't a member sees **Koden vises bare for medlemmer.** (The code is only shown to members.) and **Bli medlem** (Become a member).
- The member pass is under **Medlemskort** (Member pass).

The membership texts and partner logos show in set places on biso.no. See [Partners and membership texts](/guide/members-and-benefits/partners-and-texts).

## What the system does on its own

- Puts a **Planlagt** (Scheduled) benefit live on its start date, and takes a benefit down after its end date.
- Tells biso.no to update after each change to a benefit, partner, logo or membership text. If biso.no doesn't answer, it catches up within a few minutes.
- Writes English text for you. For a benefit, it does this in the background when **Oversett automatisk** (Translate automatically) is on. For a membership text, it fills in each English line you left empty when you save.
- Copies the members list from 24SevenOffice each time the sync runs. The list shows when it was last refreshed.
- Checks every scanned pass with 24SevenOffice, and gives a grey **Kunne ikke sjekke — prøv igjen** (Couldn't check — try again) if it can't.
- Ends guest scanner links at the time set on them, and scanner access at its end date.
- Sends the scanner invitation email when you give someone scanner access.
- Writes changes to benefits, partners, logos, membership texts and scanner access to the activity log.

It never sends:

- a push notification or an email about a new, changed or ended benefit
- an email when scanner access is revoked or ends
- a message to a member when their pass is scanned

## What you do yourself

- Publish benefits, and keep their codes, links and dates right.
- Hide or delete offers that have stopped, and end partnerships.
- Make a guest scanner link for each event, and revoke it when you're done.
- Give scanner access, and revoke it when the person stops scanning. Tell them yourself, because no email goes out.
- Keep the membership texts up to date.
- Ask a global admin to refresh the members list if you need it updated now.
