A big event is a trip or festival with many participants, like Winter Games or Fadderullan. It collects everyone with a ticket in one list. You can see who is coming, which room they have, and how to reach them.

In the admin, big events are in the sidebar as **Store arrangementer** (Big events), next to **Arrangementer** (Events). A normal event is a page students read. A big event is a list of people you work with.

The screens follow your admin language. This guide gives the Norwegian label first and the English in brackets.

## What you can do

- Create a big event and keep its settings.
- Import the ticket list and the hosts' sheet.
- See how you can reach each person.
- Connect each ticket to a student's BI account, so the student sees the big event in the BISO app.

## Who can use big events

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Global admins (National + Operations Unit) | Every big event, every participant, all settings. |
| Members of the board that runs the big event | The same, for that big event. |
| Campus admins | Only the participants of their own campus. They can change a big event's settings only if it is a big event on their own campus. |

- Campus admins never see participants from another campus, or participants with no campus. Only global admins and the board see those, so every person on the list needs a campus. The list tells you how many people have no campus.
- A campus admin can import only their own campus's people. Rows for another campus are left out, and the preview says so.
- Only global admins can move a big event to another campus.

If you think you should have access and don't, ask IT to check your groups.

## Create a big event

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a new big event">

Open **Store arrangementer** (Big events) and choose **Nytt stort arrangement** (New big event).

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the basics">

Fill in **Navn på norsk** (Name in Norwegian), **Campus**, **Styret som står bak** (Board that runs it), **Starter** (Starts) and **Slutter** (Ends). **Navn på engelsk** (Name in English), **Sted** (Place), **Adresse** (Address) and **Forsidebilde** (Cover image) are optional. You can change all of it later under **Innstillinger** (Settings).

Two switches are settings for the event:

- **Reise (busser)** (Travel (buses)): on if participants travel together by bus. It must be on before you can import people into buses.
- **Innsjekk for andre grupper** (Check-in for other groups): on if leaders take attendance in groups other than buses.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose a status">

**Status** is **Utkast** (Draft), **Publisert** (Published) or **Arkivert** (Archived). Connected participants see the big event while it is published. Archived hides it everywhere. Connect emails go out only for a published big event.

</Step>
<Step title="Create it">

Choose **Opprett arrangementet** (Create big event). Next, import the ticket list. See [Import people and connect tickets](/guide/big-events/import-and-connect).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Who you can reach

Above the participants list, **Hvem du når** (Who you can reach) shows three counts next to **Alle** (Everyone). Each one is a filter.

| Pile | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Får pushvarsler** (Gets push notifications) | Connected, and has push notifications on. They get a push on their phone. |
| **Tilkoblet, får e-post** (Connected, gets email) | Connected to a BI account, but without push notifications. They get an email. |
| **Ikke tilkoblet ennå, får e-post** (Not connected yet, gets email) | The ticket isn't linked to an account. They get an email to the address on the ticket. |

Everyone is in one of the three, so you can reach the whole list.

## What the system does on its own

- It links a ticket to an account when the ticket's email belongs to a BI account and that email is on exactly one ticket.
- It sends the connect emails you queue, a few at a time, and one reminder 4 days later to people who haven't connected.
- It deletes personal data 30 days after the big event ends: ticket emails, phone numbers and the connections to accounts.

## Common mistakes

- Importing a list where the people have no campus. Campus admins won't see them. Choose a campus in the import, or edit the person.
- Expecting a connect email for a draft. Emails are sent only for a published big event.
- Treating the membership warning as a block. See [Membership warning](/guide/big-events/import-and-connect#membership-warning).
