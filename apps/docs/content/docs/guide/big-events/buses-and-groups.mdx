<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- The big event exists and **Reise (busser)** (Travel (buses)) is on in its settings. Without it you can't add buses.
- The people are on the list. See [Import people and connect tickets](/guide/big-events/import-and-connect).
- Every bus belongs to a campus, and every bus needs a seat count before it can be filled.
- Campus admins work only with their own campus. Publishing the bus lists is for whoever can change the big event's settings.
- The screens follow your admin language. Each label is given in Norwegian first, with the English in brackets.

</BeforeYouStart>

## The Groups tab

Open the big event and go to **Grupper** (Groups). It has one section per kind: **Busser** (Buses), **Rom** (Rooms), **Faddergrupper** (Buddy groups), **Lag** (Teams) and **Andre** (Other). Each section has its own button, such as **Legg til buss** (Add bus) or **Legg til rom** (Add room). A group with no campus sits under **Uten campus** (No campus).

Choose the menu on a group to edit it with **Rediger** (Edit), or delete it with **Slett** (Delete). Deleting a group takes everyone out of it. The people stay on the list.

## Add a bus

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the form">

Choose **Legg til buss** (Add bus). Fill in **Navn** (Name) and **Campus**. A bus needs a campus.

</Step>
<Step title="Add the seats">

Under **Seter** (Seats), write the seat count. **La stå tomt for ingen grense** (Leave empty for no limit) is allowed when you save, but the automatic fill can't use a bus with no seat count. It asks you to add one first.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill in the way out and the way home">

**Utreise** (Out) and **Hjemreise** (Home) each have **Avgang** (Leaves) with a date and a time, **Oppmøtested** (Meeting point) and **Kartlenke** (Map link). **Hjemreise** also has **Fremme ca.** (Arrives around). You can leave all of it empty and fill it in later. The date and the time go together: choose both or neither.

A bus can also have **Kontaktperson** (Contact person), **Telefon til kontaktpersonen** (Contact person's phone) and **Notat** (Note).

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Choose **Legg til** (Add). Seat counts are a warning only: if more people are on a bus than it has seats, the bus shows **Flere folk enn seter** (More people than seats), and you can still put people on it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Rooms, buddy groups and teams

- **Legg til rom** (Add room) takes a name, a campus, **Plasser** (Places) and **Romnummer** (Room number). The room number is shown to the people in the room, and you can change it here at any time.
- A room with more places than people shows **ledige plasser** (open places). Those places hold seats on the room's bus.
- A room has a bus. A room gets its bus when you fill the buses automatically, or when you move everyone named in it onto the same bus by hand. Its open places then move to that bus too. The way out and the way home each follow on their own. While the people in a room are on different buses, the room keeps the bus it had, and so does a room with nobody named in it.
- People named into a room ride its bus, if the bus belongs to their campus. Someone from another campus joins the room and keeps their own bus.
- Buddy groups, teams and other groups take a name, a campus and optional places.
- Only the name is required. To change the type or the campus of a group, take everyone out of it first: the form says **Ta alle ut av gruppen for å endre dette** (Take everyone out of the group to change this).

Rooms booked through an import are created for you. See [Room bookings](/guide/big-events/import-and-connect#room-bookings).

## Move people by hand

On a person's row in a group, the menu has **Flytt til …** (Move to…) and **Ta ut av gruppen** (Take out of the group). On the bus board you can also drag a person to a bus, or select several people and choose **Flytt til…** (Move to…) or **Ta av bussen** (Take off the bus).

- In the move box, **Hvilken vei** (Which way) is **Begge veier** (Both ways), **Bare utreise** (Only out) or **Bare hjemreise** (Only home).
- Someone you move by hand is pinned. The automatic fill leaves them where they are, and so does an import. This goes for buses, rooms and the other groups.
- To let the fill move a person again, click the pin icon on the person. Its screen-reader label is **Løsne** followed by their name (Unpin).
- If you move a person to a bus for the way out only and they have no way home yet, they get the way home on the same bus. A way home they already have stays as it is.
- Moving a person into a room puts them on the room's bus for each trip that isn't pinned. A room with only an out bus takes them home on the same bus. If their way out is pinned, a way home they have stays where it is. If they have no way home yet, it goes on their way-out bus when that bus belongs to their campus.
- **Ta av bussen** (Take off the bus) doesn't keep anyone off the buses. The next automatic fill gives them a seat again.

Dragging is also possible from the keyboard: press space or enter on the handle, choose a bus with the arrow keys, and press space or enter again.

## Fill the buses automatically

Choose **Fyll bussene automatisk** (Fill buses automatically). The box shows the result before anything is saved.

- It works per campus. Pick one under **Hvilken campus** (Which campus), or **Alle campuser** (All campuses).
- It spreads the people evenly over the campus's buses and keeps everyone in a room on the same bus.
- People you moved by hand stay where they are.
- People you took off a bus get a seat again. The box says **Folk du tar av en buss får plass igjen neste gang du fyller bussene.** (People you take off a bus get a seat again the next time you fill.)
- People with no campus are never placed, and the bus board doesn't show them. Give them a campus on the participants list first.
- It tells you what it couldn't do: **… har ikke plass** (… has no seat), **… trenger … seter, og ingen buss har så mange** (… needs … seats; no bus has that many), **… er delt på flere busser** (… is split across buses), and which buses need a seat count first.
- Choose **Fyll bussene** (Fill the buses) to save it.

Before the bus lists are published, a fill places everyone who isn't moved by hand again, so the result can differ from the last one. After publishing, a fill only gives a seat to people who don't have a bus. Nobody who already has a bus is moved. The box says so.

## Publish the bus lists

Choose **Publiser busslistene** (Publish bus lists). It publishes for the whole big event at once. The box says how many people will get a message about their bus.

- Publishing doesn't show buses to anyone by itself. Students don't see buses anywhere yet.
- Everyone with a seat on a bus out is told which bus they are on, by a message about 10 minutes later. See [Automatic messages](#automatic-messages). People without a seat aren't messaged, since some travel on their own.
- You can still move people afterwards.
- There is no way to unpublish. The page then shows **Publisert** (Published) with the date.
- If publishing fails part-way, nothing is published and you can run it again.

## What happens next

- When you move someone, the move box shows **Busslistene er publisert** (The bus lists are published) and a tick box: **Gi beskjed til \{name} om endringen** (Tell … about the change). It is ticked by default. Untick it to move someone without them getting a message. This applies to bus moves and to moves onto a room that has a bus.
- A move into a room that won't change the person's bus doesn't ask. If it changes their bus anyway, they get a message.
- A person you tell gets a message about their bus about 10 minutes later. See [Automatic messages](#automatic-messages).
- A bus that is published and has people on it can't be deleted. The box says **Busslistene er publisert. Flytt folkene av bussen først.** (The bus lists are published. Move the people off this bus first.) Move everyone off, then delete it.

</WhatHappensNext>

## Importing a new list

An import follows the same rules. See [Importing again](/guide/big-events/import-and-connect#importing-again): people you moved by hand stay put, and the preview says how many get a message about their bus.

## Automatic messages

After the bus lists are published, the system sends bus messages by itself. You don't write them. They are listed in **Meldinger** (Messages) with the label **Automatisk** (Automatic). See [Send messages to participants](/guide/big-events/messages).

- **What starts them.** Each person's seat is checked about 10 minutes after it is set or changed. If someone is moved several times within those 10 minutes, they get one message, with the latest bus.
- **What must be true.** The big event must be published, **Reise (busser)** (Travel (buses)) must be on, and the bus lists must be published. A draft or archived big event sends nothing.
- **Publishing.** Publishing tells the people with a seat on a bus out. People without a seat aren't messaged.
- **Moves.** A person who is moved to another bus is told about the new bus. A person who loses their seat, or has none on their own campus's bus, is told they have no seat right now.
- **Reminders.** A reminder goes out 2 hours before each trip leaves, once per trip, to the people on that trip. A trip with nobody on it sends none.
- **Campus change.** A person you move to another campus comes off the old campus's buses. After publishing, they get a bus message.

The messages go by push, or by email to people without the app. The texts follow the person's language.

### Switches and texts

Open **Innstillinger** (Settings) and find **Automatiske meldinger** (Automatic messages). It shows when the big event is published and Travel is on. There are three switches:

- **Bussen din** (Your bus): when you publish, each person is told which bus they are on.
- **Bussendringer** (Bus changes): after publishing, people who are moved to another bus, or lose their seat, are told.
- **Påminnelse før avgang** (Departure reminder): two hours before a bus leaves, the people on it get a reminder.

The message about having no seat follows **Bussendringer** (Bus changes) for someone who was told about a bus before. For someone who never had a seat, it follows **Bussen din** (Your bus). With a switch off, nobody is messaged for it.

Each switch has a text under **Egen tekst** (Your own text), for example **Tekst: bussen din** (Text: your bus), **Tekst: ny buss** (Text: new bus), **Tekst: ingen bussplass** (Text: no bus seat) and **Tekst: påminnelse før avgang** (Text: departure reminder). Write your own title and text in Norwegian and English, using the buttons for personal details. Leave a field empty to use the standard text. **Tilbakestill til standard** (Reset to default) brings the standard text back.

## What the student sees

Students don't see their bus anywhere on a page or in a list yet. They get the message as a push, or as an email if they don't have the app.

## Common mistakes

- Running the automatic fill with a bus that has no seat count. Add the seats first.
- Adding a bus without a campus, or on the wrong campus. A bus is filled only with its own campus's people.
- Expecting the fill to move people who already have a bus after publishing. It only seats people without one.
- Expecting **Ta av bussen** (Take off the bus) to keep someone off the buses. The next fill gives them a seat again.
- Expecting a message the moment you publish or move someone. It goes out about 10 minutes later, and several moves in that time give one message.
- Expecting bus messages from a draft big event, with Travel off, or before the bus lists are published. Nothing is sent.
- Trying to delete a published bus with people on it. Move them off first.
