<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- The big event exists. See [How big events work](/guide/big-events).
- You have the ticket list as an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file up to 4 MB, or you can copy the rows from a spreadsheet. It can be the ticket seller's export or a hosts' sheet, as it is. You don't need to reformat it.
- To send connect emails, the big event must be **Publisert** (Published).
- Students connect with a BI account.
- The screens follow your admin language. Each label is given in Norwegian first, with the English in brackets.

</BeforeYouStart>

## Import the list

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the import">

Open the big event, go to **Deltakere** (Participants) and choose **Importer liste** (Import list).

</Step>
<Step title="Upload the file or paste rows">

Drop the file in the box, or choose **Eller lim inn rader** (Or paste rows) and paste the rows with their headings. Choose **Les listen** (Read the list). Nothing is saved yet.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the columns">

**Sjekk kolonnene** (Check the columns) shows each column with a few examples from your file and our guess at what it holds: first name, last name, full name, email, phone, campus, order number, ticket type, or a room, bus or group. Change anything that's wrong, and choose **Ikke ta med denne kolonnen** (Ignore this column) for a column you don't need.

You must choose a name column, and an email or phone column, before you can go on.

Under **Noen valg** (A few choices) you may be asked:

- **Hvilken campus hører disse personene til?** (Which campus are these people from?) Used only for rows that don't name a campus. If the file names some campuses, this is **Campus for rader som ikke sier det** (Campus for rows that don't say).
- **Hva står i kolonnen …?** (What is in the column …?) For a room, bus or group column, choose **Rom** (Room), **Buss** (Bus), **Faddergruppe** (Buddy group) or **Lag** (Team). People with the same value end up together. Buses need **Reise** (Travel) turned on in the big event's settings.
- **Hver rad er en rombestilling** (Each row is a room booking). Tick this when the file lists whoever booked a room, as the ticket seller's export does. Each buyer gets a room with the size on the ticket, and the other places stay open until the roommates are on the list. A ticket type like "2 x … 4-Person" gives two rooms.

Choose **Sjekk importen** (Check the import).

</Step>
<Step title="Check and save">

**Sjekk og lagre** (Check and save) shows how many people are new, updated or unchanged, and how many rooms or groups it will create. It also lists skipped rows and rooms with more people than places. Read it, then choose **Lagre** (Save).

</Step>
</Steps>

### Room bookings

When each row is a room booking, the file with the buyers (Payment 1) puts each buyer in a room with open places. A later file with the roommates (Payment 2) places each person in a room in this order:

1. By the order number, if their row has the same one as the booking.
2. By the buyer's email or phone, if it matches exactly one booking.
3. Otherwise they stay on the list as not in a room.

**Ikke på rom ennå** (Not in a room yet) is a filter on the participants list, with a count. To place these people, fix the file and import again.

### Importing again

- Importing a newer file updates the people already on the list. It doesn't make copies.
- People on the list who aren't in the new file are shown as **På listen, men ikke i denne filen** (On the list but not in this file). This is normal when a file covers only some people, like the buyers. Nobody is removed automatically. To remove someone, choose **Fjern** (Remove) on their row.
- A person already in another room or group of the same kind is moved to the one in the new file. The preview counts these, for example "3 personer bytter rom" (3 people change room).
- Someone you moved by hand, to a bus, a room or another group, stays where you put them, whatever the file says. The preview counts them, for example "2 personer ble flyttet for hånd og blir der" (2 people stay where they were moved by hand). A person you moved by hand to a bus for the way out takes the way home with them: their way home is on that bus too.
- A person in a room that has a bus rides that bus, unless you moved them to another bus by hand for that trip.
- A booked room gets the number of places on its ticket again, if the ticket now says another size.
- After the bus lists are published, everyone whose bus changes is marked to get a message about their bus. The preview says how many, for example "3 personer får en melding om bussen sin" (3 people will get a message about their bus). The import only marks them.
- A new email address for someone already on the list stops the link in the connect emails they already got. See [Send connect emails](#send-connect-emails).
- Rooms and groups belong to a campus. Two groups with the same name on different campuses stay separate.
- If **Kunne ikke lagre. Ingenting ble endret.** (Couldn't save. Nothing was changed.) shows, nothing was written. Try again.
- If **Noen rader ble ikke lagret.** (Some rows couldn't be saved.) shows, part of the file was saved. Reload the list and check it before you import again.

## The participants list

- **Legg til person** (Add person) adds someone who isn't on the ticket list. The email you give is where their connect email goes.
- **Rediger** (Edit), in the menu on a row, changes a person's name, email, phone or campus. It doesn't change their account. A new email address stops the link in the connect emails they already got.
- **Søk etter navn eller e-post** (Search by name or email) and the **Campus** filter narrow the list. The three reach piles, **Ikke på rom ennå**, **Ikke på buss ennå** (Not on a bus yet) and **Trenger ny lenke** (Needs a new link) are filters too, each with a count. **Trenger ny lenke** shows people who got a connect email, aren't connected, and whose link no longer works, for example after you changed their email address.
- The **Buss** (Bus) column shows each person's bus, with **Hjem: …** (Home: …) when the way home is on another bus, and **Ingen buss** (No bus) when they have none. To place people, see [Buses and groups](/guide/big-events/buses-and-groups).
- **Kopier e-postadresser** (Copy emails) copies the addresses of the people shown.
- **Last ned CSV** (Download CSV) downloads the people shown as a spreadsheet.

## Connect tickets to accounts

A ticket is connected when it is linked to a student's BI account. Once it is connected, the ticket belongs to their account and you can reach them through it.

### Automatic links

When you save an import, a ticket is linked by itself only if its email belongs to a BI account and that email is on exactly one ticket in the big event. A shared email, such as a parent or a friend who bought several tickets, never links automatically.

### Possible matches

When the phone number, or the name and campus, match a BI account, the row shows **Mulig treff: navn (e-post)** (Possible match: name (email)) with the reason, **samme telefon** (same phone) or **samme navn og campus** (same name and campus). Choose **Koble til** (Link) to accept it, or **Ikke denne** (Not them) to stop suggesting that account.

### Link by hand

In the menu on a person's row, choose **Koble til konto** (Link account). Search by name or email. Only accounts that have signed in with BI are listed. An account marked **Har allerede en billett her** (Already has a ticket here) can't be chosen, because an account holds one ticket per big event. A ticket can be linked to one account.

### Unlink

Choose **Koble fra konto** (Unlink account) in the row's menu to disconnect a ticket. The person stays on the list, and their account no longer shows the big event. The link in the connect emails they already got stops working, so nobody else can connect the ticket with it. To let the right person connect, send a new email with **Send på nytt** (Send again).

## Send connect emails

Choose **Send koblings-e-post** (Send connect emails) above the list. It sends to people who aren't connected and haven't had a connect email yet, and the number in brackets is how many they are. So after you import the roommates (Payment 2), it reaches only the new people, not the buyers who already got one. Before anything is sent, you're asked to confirm, with the number of emails. The button is disabled until the big event is published.

- When someone who isn't connected has already had a connect email, a second button appears: **Send på nytt til alle som ikke er tilkoblet** (Send again to everyone not connected). It sends to everyone who isn't connected, also those who already had an email, and asks first too. Everyone who already had one gets it again, so use it sparingly.
- For one person, open the menu on their row and choose **Send på nytt** (Send again). Do this after you change someone's email address or unlink them: their old link no longer works, and the new email has a new one.
- After a send, a message says how many emails were queued. **Venter allerede på å bli sendt** (Already waiting to be sent) means the emails were queued before and haven't gone out yet.
- One email goes to each address. When several people share an address, one email lists each person's own link.
- Emails go out a few at a time, so it can take a while for a long list.
- People who haven't connected get one reminder 4 days after the first email.
- The links stop working when the big event ends.

## What happens next

- The student gets the email, opens their link on biso.no, and signs in with their BI account.
- The row changes from **Tilkoblings-e-post sendt** (Connect email sent) to **Tilkoblet** (Connected) when they confirm.
- Until a ticket is connected, the person is in **Ikke tilkoblet ennå, får e-post** (Not connected yet, gets email).

</WhatHappensNext>

## What the student sees

The student's pages follow the language of biso.no. The English text is quoted here, with the Norwegian in brackets.

1. The email has a link to biso.no/connect/ followed by a code.
2. The page asks them to sign in with their BI account. If they are signed in with another account, it asks them to switch.
3. They see the name on the ticket and choose **Connect my ticket** (Koble til billetten min). If the ticket isn't theirs, the page tells them to send the link to the right person.
4. They see **You're connected!** (Nå er du koblet til!) and "Your bus, room and updates will show in the BISO app." The page also has the BISO app download buttons.

If the big event has ended, the page says **This event has ended, so the link no longer works.** (Arrangementet er over, så lenken virker ikke lenger.) and offers nothing more. If the link no longer works for another reason, the page offers **Get a new link** (Få en ny lenke). If they never got the email, they can open biso.no/connect/help, **Didn't get the email?** (Fikk du ikke e-posten?), type the address on their ticket, and get a new link. The page gives the same answer whether or not the address is on a list.

## Membership warning

The participants list can show **Mangler gyldig medlemskap** (No valid membership) on a connected person. **Sjekk medlemskap** (Check memberships) looks up everyone who is connected. It is a warning only. It doesn't stop anyone from connecting or taking part. Handle it the way your event needs, for example by asking them to join.

Only connected people can be checked, because that is how we find their membership.

## Personal data

Ticket emails, phone numbers, connections to accounts and the email history are deleted 30 days after the big event ends. After that the list is empty. Rooms and groups stay without names: a room named after its buyer gets its room number, or **Rom**, as its name, and buses and groups lose their contact person.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing the wrong column for the name or the email. Check the examples shown on **Sjekk kolonnene**.
- Importing the Payment 2 file before the Payment 1 file. The roommates have no booking to join and stay in **Ikke på rom ennå**.
- Expecting people missing from a newer file to disappear. They stay until you remove them.
- Importing again after "Noen rader ble ikke lagret" without reloading. Reload and check the list first.
- Sending connect emails while the big event is a draft. Publish it first.
- Using **Send på nytt til alle som ikke er tilkoblet** (Send again to everyone not connected) after each import. **Send koblings-e-post** (Send connect emails) already reaches the new people, without emailing the others again.
