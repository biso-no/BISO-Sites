<BeforeYouStart>

## Before you start

- The big event exists. See [How big events work](/guide/big-events).
- You have the ticket list as an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file up to 4 MB, or you can copy the rows from a spreadsheet. It can be the ticket seller's export or a hosts' sheet, as it is. You don't need to reformat it.
- To send connect emails, the big event must be **Published**.
- Students connect only with a BI account. Tickets bought with another email are still connected through a BI account.

</BeforeYouStart>

## Import the list

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the import">

Open the big event, go to **Participants** and choose **Import list**.

</Step>
<Step title="Upload the file or paste rows">

Drop the file in the box, or choose **Or paste rows** and paste the rows with their headings. Choose **Read the list**. Nothing is saved yet.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the columns">

This step is called **Check the columns**. It shows each column with a few examples from your file and our guess at what it holds: first name, last name, full name, email, phone, campus, order number, ticket type, or a room, bus or group. Change anything that's wrong, and set a column to **Ignore this column** if you don't need it.

You must choose a name column, and an email or phone column, before you can go on.

Depending on the file, you also answer a few questions:

- **Which campus are these people from?** Used only for rows that don't name a campus.
- **What is in the column?** For a room, bus or group column, say which it is. People with the same value end up together. Buses need **Travel** turned on in the big event's settings.
- **Each row is a room booking.** Tick this when the file lists whoever booked a room, as the ticket seller's export does. Each buyer gets a room with the size on the ticket, and the other places stay open until the roommates are on the list. A ticket type like "2 x … 4-Person" gives two rooms.

</Step>
<Step title="Check and save">

The preview shows how many people are new, updated or unchanged, and how many rooms or groups it will create. Read it, then choose **Save**.

</Step>
</Steps>

### Room bookings

When each row is a room booking, the file with the buyers (Payment 1) fills each room with the buyer. A later file with the roommates (Payment 2) places each person in a room in this order:

1. By the order number, if their row has the same one as the booking.
2. By the buyer's email or phone, if it matches exactly one booking.
3. Otherwise they stay on the list as **Not in a room yet**.

**Not in a room yet** is a filter on the participants list, with a count. Fix the file and import again to place them.

### Importing again

- Importing a newer file updates the people already on the list. It doesn't make copies.
- People on the list who aren't in the new file are listed in the preview as **On the list but not in this file**. This is normal when a file covers only some people, like the buyers. Nobody is removed automatically. To remove someone, use **Remove** on their row.
- A person already in another room or group of the same kind is moved to the one in the new file. The preview counts these as people who change room.
- Rooms and groups belong to a campus. Two groups with the same name on different campuses stay separate.
- If saving partly fails, you see "Some rows couldn't be saved". Reload the list and check it before you import again.

## Connect tickets to accounts

A ticket is connected when it is linked to a student's BI account. Connected students see the big event in the BISO app once it is available there.

### Automatic links

When you save an import, a ticket is linked by itself only if its email belongs to a BI account and that email is on exactly one ticket in the big event. A shared email, such as a parent or a friend who bought several tickets, never links automatically.

### Possible matches

When the phone number, or the name and campus, match a BI account, the row shows **Possible match: name (email)** with the reason. Choose **Link** to accept it, or **Not them** to stop suggesting that account.

### Link by hand

On a person's row, open the menu and choose **Link account**. Search by name or email. Only accounts that have signed in with BI are listed. An account can hold one ticket per big event, and a ticket can be linked to one account.

### Unlink

Choose **Unlink account** on the row's menu to disconnect a ticket. The person stays on the list, and their account no longer shows the big event.

## Send connect emails

Choose **Send connect emails** above the list. The button shows how many emails will go out. It is disabled until the big event is **Published**.

- The button sends to people who aren't connected yet. For one person, open the menu on their row and choose **Send again**.
- One email goes to each address. When several people share an address, one email lists each person's own link.
- Emails go out a few at a time, so it can take a while for a long list.
- People who haven't connected get one reminder 4 days after the first email.
- The links stop working when the big event ends.

<WhatHappensNext>

## What happens next

- The student gets the email, opens their link on biso.no, and signs in with their BI account.
- The row changes from **Connect email sent** to **Connected** when they confirm.
- Where a ticket isn't connected, the person still gets email from you.

</WhatHappensNext>

## What the student sees

1. The email has a link to biso.no/connect/ followed by a code.
2. The page asks them to sign in with their BI account. If they are signed in with another account, it asks them to switch.
3. They see the name on the ticket and choose **Connect my ticket**. If the ticket isn't theirs, the page tells them to send the link to the right person.
4. They see **You're connected!** with the BISO app download buttons.

If the link no longer works, the page offers **Get a new link**. If they never got the email, they can open biso.no/connect/help (**Didn't get the email?**), type the address on their ticket, and get a new link. The page gives the same answer whether or not the address is on a list.

## Membership warning

The participants list can show **No valid membership** on a connected person. **Check memberships** looks up everyone who is connected. It is a warning only. It doesn't stop anyone from connecting or taking part. Handle it the way your event needs, for example by asking them to join.

Only connected people can be checked, because that is how we find their membership.

## Personal data

Ticket emails, phone numbers, connections to accounts and the email history are deleted 30 days after the big event ends. After that the list is empty.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing the wrong column for the name or the email. Check the examples shown on **Check the columns**.
- Importing the Payment 2 file before the Payment 1 file. The roommates have no booking to join and stay **Not in a room yet**.
- Expecting people missing from a newer file to disappear. They stay until you remove them.
- Importing again after a failed save without reloading. Reload and check the list first.
- Sending connect emails while the big event is a draft. Publish it first.
