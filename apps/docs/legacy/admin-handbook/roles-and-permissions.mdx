# Roles and permissions

Your role in the admin app is not set inside the app. It is **derived** from the Azure AD security groups your BI account belongs to. When you sign in, the app reads those groups and grants you the matching role automatically.

There are three roles.

## The three roles

### Global admin

Full access to everything across every campus. Typically BISO IT and the national operations unit.

You are a global admin if your account is in **both**:

- `SG-App-Campus-National`
- `SG-App-Dept-OperationsUnit`

Global admins can manage settings, IT users, every campus's content, and every approval queue.

### Campus admin

Full access to one or more campuses. Typically the leadership team for Oslo, Bergen, Trondheim, or Stavanger.

You are a campus admin for a campus if your account is in **both**:

- `SG-App-Campus-{City}` (for example `SG-App-Campus-Bergen`)
- `SG-App-Dept-Ledelsen{City}` (for example `SG-App-Dept-LedelsenBergen`)

You can be a campus admin for several campuses at once if you are in several pairs.

### Department

Limited access to the content your department is responsible for: jobs, events, news, and pages. Typically PR, HR, Finance, Marketing, or any other BISO unit lead.

You have the department pseudo-role if your account is in **any** `SG-App-Dept-*` security group (other than `OperationsUnit`, which makes you a global admin instead).

Department users do not see shop, benefits, the documents library, submissions, approvals, drafts, or activity — those are administrative views, not editorial ones.

## How scope works

Roles also control **which records** you see, not just which screens.

- A **global admin** sees everything by default and can [scope themselves to a single campus](/docs/admin-handbook/campus-context) using the campus switcher in the sidebar.
- A **campus admin** is fixed to the campus(es) they manage. Lists, dashboards, and approvals only show that campus's data.
- A **department** user sees content for their department across whichever campus it belongs to, but cannot manage campus-wide settings.

## Things this app does **not** use

A few notes for people who have worked with the old setup:

- Roles are **not** stored as Appwrite user labels. Even if a label is set, the app ignores it for role decisions.
- The old `SG-App-Role-*` groups (finance, hr, pr, controller) are no longer used. Only the `SG-App-Campus-*` and `SG-App-Dept-*` groups matter.

For the technical detail of how this all hangs together, see [Auth and roles](/docs/architecture/auth-and-roles).
