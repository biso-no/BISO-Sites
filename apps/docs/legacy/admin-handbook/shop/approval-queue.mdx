# Approval queue

Not every product appears on the storefront the moment it's created. Some listings — especially second-hand items submitted by students and anything above a certain price — wait in the approval queue until a campus admin reviews them. This screen is where that review happens.

Find it in the sidebar under **Shop → Approval queue** (`/shop/approval-queue`).

## Who uses it

| Role | What they can do |
|------|------------------|
| Global admin | Approve or reject anything, across all campuses. |
| Campus admin | Approve or reject items belonging to their campus. |
| Department lead | No access — the queue isn't visible in the sidebar. |

If you're a campus admin and an item is sitting in the queue for another campus, you won't be able to act on it. That's intentional.

## What's in the queue

Any product whose status is `pending_approval` shows up here. That includes:

- Second-hand listings submitted by students through the public site.
- High-value items where someone (a department, a campus admin, or BISO itself) wants a second pair of eyes before it goes live.
- Anything created with explicit approval-required flags.

Products with status `available`, `sold`, `reserved`, or `inactive` are **not** in the queue — they are already live (or have been taken down) and are managed from the regular [Products](/docs/admin-handbook/shop/products) list.

## What to look at before approving

The queue is a quality gate, not a rubber stamp. Before you approve a listing, take a minute to check:

| Check | What to look for |
|-------|------------------|
| **Title and description** | Clear, not misleading, free of profanity, in correct Norwegian or English. |
| **Price** | Reasonable for the item. Wildly underpriced or overpriced items are usually a mistake or a test listing. |
| **Images** | At least one image, showing the actual item. No copyrighted material, no screenshots of other shops. |
| **Condition** | Honest. A "like_new" T-shirt with visible stains in the photo is a "good" at best. |
| **Ledger account number** | Filled in. Without it, accounting will have to fix it after the fact. If the field is blank, do not approve — open the product, set the account code (ask finance if you're unsure), then come back. |
| **Seller info** | If it's a student listing, there should be a name and a contact method. |

## Approving or rejecting

Each row in the queue has two buttons:

- **Approve** — the product's status flips from `pending_approval` to `available` and it appears on the storefront within seconds.
- **Reject** — the product is marked inactive. The submitter (if there is one) gets a notification telling them it wasn't approved. You can write a short reason for the rejection — please do, it saves a back-and-forth later.

If you're not sure, you can leave a product in the queue and ask the submitter to fix something specific (a better photo, the missing account number) before you come back. The item stays in `pending_approval` until you act.

## After approval

An approved product behaves exactly like one created directly:

- It's visible on biso.no/shop immediately.
- It can be bought, paid for, and posted to 24SevenOffice.
- It can be edited later from the regular product editor — see [Products](/docs/admin-handbook/shop/products).

If you approve something by mistake, open the product and switch its **Status** to `inactive`. That removes it from the storefront without losing the data.

<Callout type="info">
The approval queue is one of the few places where you'll regularly handle content submitted by students rather than by staff. Be a little stricter than you would be with internal listings — the storefront is the public face of BISO.
</Callout>
