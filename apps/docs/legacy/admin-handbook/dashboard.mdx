# Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen you land on after signing in. It is a one-page summary of what's happening across the admin app and a quick way to jump into the work that needs your attention. It is available to global admins and campus admins.

## What you see

The dashboard is built from a few key panels.

### KPIs at the top

A row of high-level numbers — for example total published pages, upcoming events, open jobs, and current news posts. The numbers respect your campus context: if you are a campus admin or a global admin scoped to a campus, you see that campus's numbers only.

### Drafts waiting on you

A count of pages, jobs, and events currently sitting in **draft**. Clicking the panel takes you to the [Drafts](/docs/admin-handbook/drafts) screen so you can pick up where someone left off.

### Approvals queue

A count of approval requests that are pending and that you are allowed to approve. Clicking takes you to [Approvals](/docs/admin-handbook/approvals). If you don't approve anything yourself, this count stays at zero.

### Publishing stats

A short summary of how many things have been published recently — usually broken down by content type. Useful for spotting weeks where nothing went out.

### Recent activity

A condensed feed of the last few changes across the system: who published what, who created which job, who archived which submission. For the full feed, open [Activity](/docs/admin-handbook/activity).

## Things to know

- The dashboard is read-only. You won't accidentally publish anything from this screen — every panel links into the area where the actual work happens.
- All counts respect your role and campus. A Bergen campus admin will never see Oslo numbers.
- The data comes from the same tables that drive the rest of the app (pages, events, jobs, news, audit logs), so a number on the dashboard always matches the list view it links to.

## When the dashboard is empty

A freshly set up campus, or a campus you have just switched to, may have very low numbers. That is expected. It is not a bug. Open one of the content sections directly (Pages, Jobs, Events) to add the first record.
