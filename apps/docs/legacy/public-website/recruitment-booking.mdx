# Recruitment booking

When HR wants a candidate to choose their own interview time, they generate a booking link in the admin app and send it to the candidate. The link lands on `/recruitment/book/[token]`, a page that needs no account: the token in the URL is the only credential.

## What the candidate sees

1. The role and campus they applied to.
2. A list of slots where every panel member is free, in Oslo office hours (09:00–17:00, weekdays), starting at least two hours from now.
3. They pick one and confirm.
4. A confirmation that says whether a calendar invite with the Teams link has been sent to their address.

## How slots are computed

The booking token (`recruitment_booking_tokens`) stores:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| `window_from`, `window_to` | The range the interview may fall in. |
| `duration_minutes` | Slot length, chosen by HR when issuing the link. |
| `panel_user_ids` | The panel as JSON: user id, email, name, and who leads. |

`getBookingContext(token)` reads the panel's busy intervals from Microsoft Graph and runs them through `availableSlots` (`@repo/shared/recruitment/booking-slots`), which steps through the window every 30 minutes and keeps the starts that fall in office hours and overlap nobody's calendar. Without Graph credentials the page falls back to plain office hours and says so.

## Confirming

`confirmBookingSlot(token, startsAt)` recomputes the slots and refuses a start that is no longer free, claims the token atomically so two confirms cannot both book, and then:

1. Creates the `job_interviews` row (Round 1, location Teams, length from the token) with the candidate and the panel as `job_interview_participants`.
2. Creates the Teams meeting and the Outlook invite from the lead panelist's mailbox via `@repo/connectors/azure/recruitment-scheduling`, and stores the meeting link and event id on the interview.
3. Marks the token consumed.

The response carries `invited`, which the page uses to word the confirmation honestly.

## Tokens

Tokens are random, HMAC-signed with `RECRUITMENT_BOOKING_SECRET`, stored only as a SHA-256 hash, single-use, and expire with the window (14 days by default). An invalid, used or expired token shows an error and tells the candidate to contact BISO.

## Privacy and SEO

The route sets `robots: noindex` and is left out of the sitemap. The page shows the candidate nothing they did not already know about themselves.

## For developers

The token is the only credential on this page, so: keep tokens long and random (they are 32 random bytes plus an HMAC), never log them, and keep the page from exposing anything the candidate does not already know. Each page load reads the panel's calendars from Graph; if the route ever becomes a target, put a rate limit in front of it.

## Environment

Both apps read the same Microsoft Graph credentials: `AZURE_GRAPH_CLIENT_ID` (or `AZURE_APP_ID`), `AZURE_GRAPH_CLIENT_SECRET`, `AZURE_GRAPH_TENANT_ID` (or `AZURE_TENANT_ID`). The web app needs them too, because the candidate's confirmation sends the invite.
