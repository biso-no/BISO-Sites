# Getting Started

There are two ways to read these docs depending on what brought you here.

## Pick a starting point

### "I'm administering the platform"

You log in to **admin.biso.no** to do things like publish a page, post a job,
manage the shop, or review applications. You may be a campus admin, a
department lead, or part of the national team.

→ Read [**Platform overview**](/docs/getting-started/platform-overview) to
understand what you're working with, then [**For administrators**](/docs/getting-started/for-administrators)
for the quickest path into the [Admin Handbook](/docs/admin-handbook).

### "I'm taking over the code"

You're the new IT Manager, an external consultant, or an internal developer
who needs to ship a change.

→ Read [**Platform overview**](/docs/getting-started/platform-overview) and
[**Key concepts**](/docs/getting-started/concepts), then jump to
[**For developers**](/docs/getting-started/for-developers) which routes you
to setup, architecture, and the handover docs.

## Don't skip these

Whoever you are, two pages will save you time:

1. [**Key concepts**](/docs/getting-started/concepts) — what a "campus",
   "department", "page", "translation ref", and "draft document" actually
   mean in this system. The vocabulary is shared by admins and developers.

2. [**Glossary**](/docs/getting-started/glossary) — quick lookup for
   acronyms (BISO, M365, SG, AAD, RSC), table names, and product names you
   will run into constantly.
