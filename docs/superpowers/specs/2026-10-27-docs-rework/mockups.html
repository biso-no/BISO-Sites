How do I send a booking link to a candidate? From the platform guide

Open the vacancy, go to the candidate, and create a booking link:

  1. In Jobs, open the vacancy and choose Pipeline.
  2. Open the candidate and choose Create booking link.
  3. Copy the link and email it to the candidate yourself. The system doesn't send it.
The link works once and expires after 14 days. Booking creates the Teams interview but doesn't move the candidate to Interview, so do that by hand.
