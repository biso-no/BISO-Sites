Building blocks organizers can add in admin

Click the ones you think they'll need. Leave out anything that feels like too much.

🗓

Programme

Day by day, with times and places. Powers "Now / Next" and "Today".

📣

Updates

Short messages that also go out as push or email. Can be marked Important.

✅

Before you go checklist

Things to do or pack. Students tick them off on their own phone.

ℹ️

Practical info pages

Free text with headings and pictures: packing list, rules, ski rental, FAQ.

🗺

Map

An uploaded picture of the venue, plus a link to open the address in Maps.

☎️

Contacts

Organizers, bus leads, and an emergency number, with tap to call.

🔗

Links and buttons

Feedback form, Tickster, ski rental shop, anything external.
